Creativity1 份量表↗ 在理論模型裡看這個變數

變數群：績效與成果　›　個人績效　用到它的理論：BBTNFCCR

Zhou & George (2001)

Zhou & George (2001) Academy of Management Journal 44(4), 682–696, employee creativity (13 items, supervisor-rated; 部分題承 Scott & Bruce 1994) ↗

  1. Suggests new ways to achieve goals or objectives.
  2. Comes up with new and practical ideas to improve performance.
  3. Searches out new technologies, processes, techniques, and/or product ideas.
  4. Suggests new ways to increase quality.
  5. Is a good source of creative ideas.
  6. Often has new and innovative ideas.
  7. Comes up with creative solutions to problems.
  8. Suggests new ways of performing work tasks.
量尺：5-point, 1 = not at all characteristic, 5 = very characteristic（主管評）
信度：α = .96（Zhou & George 2001，依常見引用）
原始情境：
注意：開過的頁面把 13 題歸給 Scott & Bruce (1994)；實際上 Zhou & George (2001) 的 13 題版是最常引用的主管評創造力量表。客觀替代：Torrance TTCT、Guilford 替代用途測驗（流暢、變通、獨創）。
