別名：Employee Satisfaction

變數群：滿意與不滿　›　滿意　用到它的理論：AEqTGSTIPCBSPCSCOJLMXPOSTStressTCMHerzberg

Minnesota Satisfaction Questionnaire — Short Form（MSQ-SF，20 題）

Weiss, D. J., Dawis, R. V., England, G. W., & Lofquist, L. H.（1967）。Manual for the Minnesota Satisfaction Questionnaire。Minnesota Studies in Vocational Rehabilitation，22。Minneapolis: University of Minnesota ↗

員工對工作各個面向（工作本身、主管、同事、薪酬、升遷、工作條件等）的滿意程度；可分內在滿意、外在滿意與整體滿意。

  1. Being able to keep busy all the time
  2. The chance to work alone on the job
  3. The chance to do different things from time to time
  4. The chance to be “somebody” in the community
  5. The way my boss handles his/her workers
  6. The competence of my supervisor in making decisions
  7. Being able to do things that do not go against my conscience
  8. The way my job provides for steady employment
  9. The chance to do things for other people
  10. The chance to tell people what to do
  11. The chance to do something that makes use of my abilities
  12. The way company policies are put into practice
  13. My pay and the amount of work I do
  14. The chances for advancement on this job
  15. The freedom to use my own judgment
  16. The chance to try my own methods of doing the job
  17. The working conditions
  18. The way my co-workers get along with each other
  19. The praise I get for doing a good job
  20. The feeling of accomplishment I get from the job
量尺：5-point（Very Dissatisfied / Dissatisfied / Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied / Satisfied / Very Satisfied；題幹「On my present job, this is how I feel about…」）
信度：α = .935（20 題整體；羅馬尼亞私立醫療機構員工，Pîrvu et al. 2026 驗證研究）；該研究引述波蘭研究 .892、希臘裔塞浦路斯研究 .955
原始情境：美國職業復健研究；一般員工對現職的滿意
注意：題項逐字取自 PMC 文章附錄 Table A1 的英文原題欄（羅馬尼亞語譯文並列）。原手冊將 20 題分為 intrinsic（常見計分為第 1–4、7–11、15、16、20 題）與 extrinsic（5、6、12、13、14、19）兩分量表與 general satisfaction（全 20 題），但本次未打開手冊，分配請以 Vocational Psychology Research（University of Minnesota）官方版為準；官方網站 https://vpr.psych.umn.edu/node/26 需授權取得量表。短版是從長版 20 個分量表各取相關最高的一題而成。羅馬尼亞研究 EFA 得到三因素（Task Enrichment、Autonomy Satisfaction、Supervisory Relationships）並建議刪 8 題，顯示內／外在二分在非美國樣本不一定成立。

Brayfield–Rothe Index of Job Satisfaction（18 題）

Brayfield, A. H., & Rothe, H. F.（1951）。An index of job satisfaction。Journal of Applied Psychology，35(5)，307–311 ↗

員工對工作整體的態度／感受（overall job satisfaction），不分面向。

  1. My job is like a hobby to me.
  2. My job is usually interesting enough to keep me from getting bored.
  3. It seems that my friends are more interested in their jobs.
  4. I consider my job rather unpleasant.
  5. I enjoy my work more than my leisure time.
  6. I am often bored with my job.
  7. I feel fairly well satisfied with my present job.
  8. Most of the time I have to force myself to go to work.
  9. I am satisfied with my job for the time being.
  10. I feel that my job is no more interesting than others I could get.
  11. I definitely dislike my work.
  12. I feel that I am happier in my work than most other people.
  13. Most days I am enthusiastic about my work.
  14. Each day of work seems like it will never end.
  15. I like my job better than the average worker does.
  16. My job is pretty uninteresting.
  17. I find real enjoyment in my work.
  18. I am disappointed that I ever took this job.
量尺：5-point Likert（Strongly Agree / Agree / Undecided / Disagree / Strongly Disagree）；總分 18–90
信度：奇偶折半 r = .77，Spearman-Brown 校正後 .87（1951 年原始樣本：231 位女性辦公室員工）
原始情境：美國辦公室員工與夜校學生
注意：題項取自網路彙整頁（二手整理，原頁另列一題不計分的範例題「There are some conditions concerning my job that could be improved.」）。正向題為第 1, 2, 5, 7, 9, 12, 13, 15, 17 題，其餘 9 題反向計分（依 Hindi 改編版 JSS-18 的計分說明， https://ijip.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/18.01.368.20231103.pdf ）。原文以 Thurstone 法從 246 題篩選。最常用的縮短版是 Judge et al. 1998 的 5 題版（見下一筆）。

Brayfield–Rothe 5 題縮短版（Judge et al. 1998）

Judge, T. A., Locke, E. A., Durham, C. C., & Kluger, A. N.（1998）。Dispositional effects on job and life satisfaction: The role of core evaluations。Journal of Applied Psychology，83(1)，17–34 ↗

整體工作滿意（overall job satisfaction）的情感式自評。

  1. I feel fairly well satisfied with my present job.
  2. Most days I am enthusiastic about my work.
  3. Each day of work seems like it will never end.
  4. I find real enjoyment in my work.
  5. I consider my job rather unpleasant.
量尺：5-point Likert（strongly agree – strongly disagree）；部分研究用 7 點
信度：原文 α 未在打開的來源取得；土耳其驗證研究（N = 253）單因素、α = .929（搜尋結果轉述，未打開原文）
原始情境：美國與以色列員工的核心自我評價研究
注意：五題逐字沿用 Brayfield & Rothe 1951 原題；第 3 題「Each day of work seems like it will never end」與第 5 題「I consider my job rather unpleasant」反向計分。QuestionPro 範本頁只列前四題且第一題印成「I feel fairly satisfied with my present job」，此處依 1951 原題。Judge 的官方量表頁 http://www.timothy-judge.com/JS2.htm 本次被 Cloudflare 擋下未能打開。Thompson & Phua（2012）另提出 4 題 Brief Index of Affective Job Satisfaction（BIAJS），本次未取得題項。

Michigan Organizational Assessment Questionnaire — Job Satisfaction Subscale（MOAQ-JSS，3 題）

Cammann, C., Fichman, M., Jenkins, D., & Klesh, J.（1983）。Assessing the attitudes and perceptions of organizational members。In S. E. Seashore, E. E. Lawler, P. H. Mirvis, & C. Cammann（Eds.），Assessing organizational change: A guide to methods, measures, and practices（pp. 71–138）。New York: Wiley ↗

整體工作滿意（global job satisfaction）。

  1. All in all, I am satisfied with my job
  2. In general, I don't like my job
  3. In general, I like working here
量尺：7-point Likert（strongly disagree – strongly agree；依網路彙整頁，本次未打開原書）
信度：各研究 α 介於 .67–.95；Bowling & Hammond（2008）後設分析樣本加權平均內部一致性 .84（以上皆為搜尋結果轉述，未打開原文）
原始情境：美國組織變革評估（密西根大學 ISR 品質工作研究計畫，1970 年代中期）
注意：題項逐字取自 QIC-WD 的 Job Satisfaction Umbrella Summary（該文件把出處標為 Lawler, Cammann, Nadler & Jenkins 1975 的 MOAQ 進度報告；多數文獻引 Cammann et al. 1983 Table 4-2, p. 84）。第 2 題反向計分。Bowling & Hammond 2008（Journal of Vocational Behavior 73, 63–77）的後設分析支持其與工作壓力源、組織知覺等的構念效度。優點是極短，適合放進長問卷；缺點是三題中有一題問「working here」偏組織而非工作本身。Paul Spector 的量表庫頁 https://paulspector.com/assessments/assessment-archive/job-attitudes/michigan-organizational-assessment-questionnaire-moaq/ 提供 docx 原檔。

Smith, P. C., Kendall, L. M., & Hulin, C. L.（1969）。The measurement of satisfaction in work and retirement: A strategy for the study of attitudes。Chicago: Rand McNally ↗

對工作五個面向（工作本身、薪資、升遷機會、主管、同事）的描述式滿意；受訪者不是直接說多滿意，而是勾選形容詞是否描述該面向。

量尺：Adjective checklist：每個形容詞勾 Y（描述）／N（不描述）／?（不確定）；正向詞 Y = 3、? = 1、N = 0，負向詞反向（計分規則依 JDI 手冊，本次未打開手冊）
信度：α：work .83–.94、promotion .81–.95、co-workers .82–.96，supervision 兩個研究亦 > .80；pay 在兩個研究低於 .80（Brannick, Kelly & McCarthy 1983，四個愛爾蘭樣本）
原始情境：美國一般員工；本次題項來源為 1969 年原版用於愛爾蘭樣本
注意：逐字題項取自 Brannick, Kelly & McCarthy（1983）。Measuring job satisfaction: The reliability of the Job Descriptive Index in diverse Irish sample populations。Irish Business and Administrative Research，5(1)，94–（ https://mural.maynoothuniversity.ie/id/eprint/21045/ ），表內列 work 18、pay 9、promotion 9、co-workers 18、supervision 18，合計 72 題；co-workers 第 2 題與第 15 題在 PDF 文字層缺漏（本次只抽出 16 題，故 co-workers 面向少 2 題）。BGSU JDI Research Group 持有版權，1985／1997／2009 年修訂版替換了若干題（例如 work 面向改含 Dull、Awful、Important、Interesting、Uses my abilities），並加上 18 題 Job in General（JIG）整體量表；另有 25 題縮短版 aJDI（每面向 5 題，Stanton et al. 2002）。改編前應向 BGSU 索取現行版與常模，不要直接用 1969 年詞表。
