John (1984) 題項未對照原文

John (1984), Journal of Marketing Research 21(3), 278–289 的 opportunism 5 題；後續 Rokkan, Heide & Wathne (2003) 等沿用

  1. On occasion, [the partner] lies about certain things in order to protect its interests.
  2. [The partner] sometimes promises to do things without actually doing them later.
  3. [The partner] sometimes alters the facts slightly in order to get what it needs.
  4. [The partner] will not always be completely honest with us.
  5. [The partner] sometimes tries to breach informal agreements to maximize its own benefit.
量尺：7-point Likert, 1 = strongly disagree, 7 = strongly agree
信度：原始研究 α 約 .80（未核對）；Kelly, Wagner & Ramsay (2018) 回顧指出各量表對 guile 的掌握不一
