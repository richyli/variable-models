Witte (1996) 題項未對照原文

Witte (1996), Journal of Health Communication 1(4), 317–342, Risk Behavior Diagnosis Scale（RBD, 12 題；susceptibility 3 題）；HBM 傳統另有 Champion (1984) 的 susceptibility 題 ↗

  1. I am at risk for [threat].
  2. It is likely that I will [get threat].
  3. It is possible that I will [get threat].
  4. [Gould 2015 改編] I believe I am seriously at risk of getting ill from smoking.
  5. [Gould 2015 改編] It is likely that I will get ill from smoking.
量尺：7-point, 1 = strongly disagree, 7 = strongly agree（Witte）；Gould 用 5 點
信度：Gould et al. (2015, N = 121) susceptibility α .55（刪一題 .62）、severity .78、total threat .79；Gould 整體 .65–1.0；法文版 12 題三因子結構穩定
