Relatedness1 份量表↗ 在理論模型裡看這個變數

變數群：需求與幸福感　›　需求滿足　用到它的理論：SDT

Deci & Ryan (2000) 題項未對照原文

Deci & Ryan (2000) / Gagné (2003) Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction Scale (BPNS, 21 題) 的 relatedness 子量表；Chen et al. (2015) BPNSFS 24 題（含 relatedness satisfaction / frustration） ↗

  1. I really like the people I interact with (BPNS)
  2. I get along with people I come into contact with (BPNS)
  3. People in my life care about me (BPNS)
  4. I feel that the people I care about also care about me (BPNSFS satisfaction)
  5. I feel connected with people who care for me, and for whom I care (BPNSFS satisfaction)
  6. I feel excluded from the group I want to belong to (BPNSFS frustration)
量尺：BPNS 7-point (1=not at all true 到 7=very true)；BPNSFS 5-point (1=completely untrue 到 5=completely true)
信度：BPNS relatedness α ≈ .80–.85；BPNSFS relatedness satisfaction α ≈ .80【未核對】
