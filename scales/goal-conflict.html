無直接的 agency-theory 量表；最接近的是 Vancouver & Schmitt (1991) 題項未對照原文

無直接的 agency-theory 量表；最接近的是 Vancouver & Schmitt (1991), Personnel Psychology 44, 333–352 的 Goal Congruence（14 個目標重要性評分的 profile 差距 D）；自陳替代為 perceived goal congruence 量表 ↗

  1. [Profile] 受訪者與主管各自評 14 項組織目標的重要性，D = sqrt(Σ(差)^2)，D 越大代表目標衝突越大
  2. [自陳替代] My goals and the goals of [principal/organization] are very similar. (R)
  3. [自陳替代] What I want to achieve in my job often conflicts with what my supervisor wants.
量尺：目標重要性 7-point, 0 = of no importance, 6 = of primary importance
信度：原始研究以校內 ICC 估計一致性，約 .24（屬跨單位一致性，不是 α）
