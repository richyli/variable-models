Grit Scale（Grit-O）Perseverance of Effort 子量表（Duckworth et al. 2007）

Duckworth, A. L., Peterson, C., Matthews, M. D., & Kelly, D. R.（2007）。Grit: Perseverance and passion for long-term goals。Journal of Personality and Social Psychology，92(6)，1087–1101。doi:10.1037/0022-3514.92.6.1087 ↗

恆毅力＝對長期目標的堅持與熱情；perseverance of effort 子向度指面對逆境仍持續努力、把事情做完的傾向

  1. I have achieved a goal that took years of work.
  2. I have overcome setbacks to conquer an important challenge.
  3. I finish whatever I begin.
  4. Setbacks don't discourage me.
  5. I am a hard worker.
  6. I am diligent.
量尺：5-point（1 = not at all like me，5 = very much like me）
信度：Study 1（1,545 位 25 歲以上成人）：總量表 α = .85，Consistency of Interests α = .84，Perseverance of Effort α = .78；Study 2 總量表 α = .79
原始情境：成人網路樣本、常春藤大學生、西點軍校學員、全國拼字比賽選手等六個研究
注意：Grit-O 共 12 題，另一子向度 Consistency of Interests 六題皆反向計分：I often set a goal but later choose to pursue a different one／New ideas and new projects sometimes distract me from previous ones／I become interested in new pursuits every few months／My interests change from year to year／I have been obsessed with a certain idea or project for a short time but later lost interest／I have difficulty maintaining my focus on projects that take more than a few months to complete。量表受 APA 版權保護。原文作者以總分為主要指標，但後設分析（Credé et al. 2017）顯示 perseverance of effort 的預測力高於 consistency，單獨用 PE 子量表測「堅持」是常見做法。Grit-S 刪掉 I have achieved a goal that took years of work 與 I have overcome setbacks to conquer an important challenge 兩題。

Short Grit Scale（Grit-S）Perseverance of Effort 四題（Duckworth & Quinn 2009）

Duckworth, A. L., & Quinn, P. D.（2009）。Development and validation of the Short Grit Scale (Grit-S)。Journal of Personality Assessment，91(2)，166–174。doi:10.1080/00223890802634290 ↗

同 Grit-O；八題短版，兩子向度各四題

  1. Setbacks don't discourage me
  2. I am a hard worker
  3. I finish whatever I begin
  4. I am diligent
量尺：5-point（1 = Not like me at all，2 = Not much like me，3 = Somewhat like me，4 = Mostly like me，5 = Very much like me）
信度：總分 α = .73–.83（發展樣本）；Perseverance of Effort α = .60–.78，Consistency of Interest α = .73–.79；Credé 等（2017）後設分析 46 個樣本平均 α 總分 .79、PE .71、CI .74；青少年一年再測 r = .68
原始情境：成人與青少年多樣本（含西點軍校、拼字比賽）
注意：Consistency of Interest 四題（反向）：New ideas and projects sometimes distract me from previous ones／I have been obsessed with a certain idea or project for a short time but later lost interest／I often set a goal but later choose to pursue a different one／I have difficulty maintaining my focus on projects that take more than a few months to complete。計分：反向題以 6 減原分，各子向度取平均。無公開常模切點。edutechwiki 另列兩版對照：https://edutechwiki.unige.ch/en/Grit

Multidimensional Persistence Scale（MPS；Howard & Crayne 2019）

Howard, M. C., & Crayne, M. P.（2019）。Persistence: Defining the multidimensional construct and creating a measure。Personality and Individual Differences，139，77–89。doi:10.1016/j.paid.2018.11.005 ↗

堅持＝為達成目標而忍受困難的傾向，分為 persistence despite difficulty（PDD）、persistence despite fear（PDF）與 inappropriate persistence（IP，為無價值目標仍持續）三向度

  1. PDD（例題）：Even if something is hard, I will keep trying at it.
  2. PDF（例題）：If something is scary, I will do it anyways.
  3. IP（例題）：I will keep trying at something, even if I know my actions are worthless.
量尺：5-point Likert（土耳其版：1 = Kesinlikle Katılmıyorum／strongly disagree，5 = Kesinlikle Katılıyorum／strongly agree）
信度：Howard & Murry（2020，TPM，27(2)，271–277）施測 15 題 MPS：PDF α = .93、PDD α = .83、IP α = .79；Gülbahar 土耳其版（13 題）：PDD .95、PDF .94、IP .80
原始情境：六個研究發展的自陳量表；後續用於成人線上樣本與土耳其大學生
注意：本次只找到三題例題（各向度一題，取自 Howard & Murry 2020 的方法節）。原文 PAID 全文與 ResearchGate 皆被擋，完整題項請回原文附錄或向作者索取。題數在不同來源不一致：Howard & Murry 說 15 題，土耳其版 13 題（PDD 第 1–5 題、PDF 第 6–10 題、IP 第 11–13 題），原文另有 Goal Time Preference 補充量表。改編時注意 IP 是「不當堅持」，與 grit 的 perseverance 方向相反，不能加總；Howard & Murry 證明 Courage Measure 與 PDF 幾乎同構（HTMT = .98）。土耳其版全文：https://acikerisim.gelisim.edu.tr/xmlui/handle/11363/6462

行為式任務堅持：無解字謎（unsolvable anagrams）與習得無助典範

Feather, N. T., & Simon, J. G.（1971）。Attribution of responsibility and valence of outcome in relation to initial confidence。Journal of Personality and Social Psychology，18(2)，173–188；Määttänen, I. 等（2021）。Evidence for a behaviourally measurable perseverance trait in humans。Behavioral Sciences，11(9)，123。doi:10.3390/bs11090123 ↗

堅持＝在無法成功或令人不快的任務上持續投入的時間或嘗試次數

  1. 客觀指標 1：Time on task —— 受試者在無解（或困難）字謎上自願花費的時間（秒），Määttänen 等指出這是文獻中最常用的堅持指標
  2. 客觀指標 2：Attempted solutions —— 測驗階段提交的解答次數（the number of submitted solutions for the test）
  3. 客觀指標 3：Proportion correct —— 可解題的正確比例（the proportion correctly solved test anagrams），用以區分能力與堅持
  4. 程序（Millisecond 版）：5 題練習字謎後接 15 題測驗字謎，其中三分之一無解，每題限時 30 秒，並告知至少答對 8 題才算通過；以練習難度 × 測驗難度做 2×2 設計
量尺：客觀行為指標（秒數、次數、比例）
信度：Määttänen 等（2021，N = 54）以六項行為任務（冷壓、握力、無解字謎、無解語文推理、穿針、無聊影片）得到兩因素結構，共解釋表現時間 37.3% 變異；無解字謎是唯一較受疲勞（depletion）而非特質解釋的任務
原始情境：實驗室個別施測；源自 Seligman 習得無助研究中「先經歷不可控失敗、再觀察是否放棄」的典範
注意：Seligman (1975) 原始論文未在本次開啟，習得無助與字謎堅持的連結取自 Millisecond 與 Määttänen 等的文獻回顧。使用時要注意：(1) 堅持時間會與疲勞、對任務可解性的判斷混淆（在明知無解的題上堅持可能是不理性而非恆毅力）；(2) 需搭配可解題以分離能力；(3) 時間上限（如 30 秒／題）會截斷分布。Behavioral Sciences 全文：https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8468401/
