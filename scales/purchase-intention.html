Willingness to buy (Dodds, Monroe & Grewal 1991)

Dodds, W. B., Monroe, K. B., & Grewal, D. (1991). Effects of price, brand, and store information on buyers' product evaluations. Journal of Marketing Research, 28(3), 307–319. ↗

在給定價格、品牌、商店資訊下，買者願意購買該產品的程度（willingness to buy）。

  1. If I were going to buy a bicycle, the probability of buying this model is
  2. The probability that I would consider buying this bicycle is
  3. At the price shown, I would consider buying the product
  4. The likelihood of purchasing this laptop is…
  5. The likelihood of ever using this laptop is…
  6. My desire to own this laptop is…
量尺：原文為 7-point 雙極量尺（very low–very high 類）；arXiv 2504.05359 的改編版用 5-point bipolar very low–very high；PMC8987512 的改編版用 7-point Likert strongly disagree–strongly agree
信度：原文數值未核對；Sweeney, Soutar & Johnson (1999) 以其中三題測 willingness-to-buy α = .95；arXiv 2504.05359 改編版三題負荷 .723–.915
原始情境：原始研究為實驗室實驗，受試者評估計算機與立體聲耳機在不同價格／品牌／商店條件下的品質、價值與購買意願
注意：本次找不到可開啟的 JMR 原文，題項來自兩個二手重製頁：前三題取自 Terblanche, Boshoff & Human-Van Eck (2022, PMC8987512) Table 7 標示的「Original three-item scale」，該表把來源合併標為 Capella et al. 2010、Dodds et al. 1991、Grewal et al. 1998a/b，「bicycle」顯示已是後續改編，第三題在該表只看到改編後句型（At the price shown, I would consider buying the … featured in the advertisement）；後三題取自 arXiv 2504.05359（Dodds 1991 量表改編到筆電）。原始五題的逐字版本請核對 JMR 原文或 Bruner 的 Marketing Scales Handbook。

Purchase Intention, 5-item semantic differential (Spears & Singh 2004)

Spears, N., & Singh, S. N. (2004). Measuring attitude toward the brand and purchase intentions. Journal of Current Issues & Research in Advertising, 26(2), 53–66. ↗

個人有意識地計畫去努力購買某品牌的程度（在 attitude-toward-the-ad 架構下與品牌態度區分）。

  1. I will never/definitely buy this brand
  2. I definitely do not intend to buy/definitely intend to buy this brand
  3. I have very low/high purchase interest
  4. I would definitely not buy/definitely buy it
  5. I would probably not/probably buy it
量尺：原文為 7-point 雙極（semantic differential）量尺；Shanbhogue & Ranjith (2024, PMC10904944) 改用 5-point Likert strongly disagree–strongly agree
信度：原文數值未核對（全文付費）；Shanbhogue & Ranjith (2024) 改編版 α = .941（全球品牌）／.945（本土品牌），CR = .952／.957
原始情境：原始研究以廣告實驗驗證品牌態度與購買意圖量表的心理計量特性；重製頁為印度消費者對全球／本土品牌
注意：題項取自 PMC10904944 Table 1 的重製（Taylor & Francis 原文頁回 403）。原量表應以雙極形容詞方式呈現（例：Definitely do not intend to buy — Definitely intend to buy），改成 Likert 同意度會失去語意差異格式。同文的品牌態度五題（unappealing/appealing、bad/good、unpleasant/pleasant、unfavorable/favorable、unlikable/likable）常與之配套使用。
