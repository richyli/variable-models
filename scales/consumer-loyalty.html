Zeithaml, Berry & Parasuraman (1996) Journal of Marketing 60(2), 31–46, behavioral intentions battery, Loyalty dimension (5 items) ↗

  1. Say positive things about XYZ to other people.
  2. Recommend XYZ to someone who seeks your advice.
  3. Encourage friends and relatives to do business with XYZ.
  4. Consider XYZ your first choice to buy services.
  5. Do more business with XYZ in the next few years.
量尺：7-point, 1 = not at all likely, 7 = extremely likely
信度：loyalty 分量表 α 多 .90 以上（後續研究）

注意：題項依常見引用改寫（頁面描述 5 題內容：正面口碑、推薦、續留、增加消費、首選）。替代：Oliver (1999) 四階段忠誠；CBBE 脈絡用 Yoo & Donthu (2001) brand loyalty 3 題。
