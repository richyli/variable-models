IS Continuance Model satisfaction (Bhattacherjee 2001)

Bhattacherjee, A. (2001). Understanding information systems continuance: An expectation-confirmation model. MIS Quarterly, 25(3), 351–370. ↗

使用者對先前使用資訊系統整體經驗的情感反應（滿意／不滿意），是持續使用意圖的主要前因。

  1. How do you feel about your overall experience of mobile banking applications use?
  2. Very dissatisfied/Very satisfied
  3. Very displeased/Very pleased
  4. Very frustrated/Very contented
  5. Absolutely terrible/Absolutely delighted
  6. [Continuance intention] I intend to continue using mobile banking applications rather than discontinue its use.
  7. [Continuance intention] My intentions are to continue using mobile banking applications than use any alternative means (traditional or web-based banking).
  8. [Continuance intention] If I could, I would like to discontinue my use of mobile banking applications (reverse coded).
  9. [Confirmation] My experience with using mobile banking applications was better than what I expected.
  10. [Confirmation] The service level provided by mobile banking applications was better than what I expected.
  11. [Confirmation] Overall, most of my expectations from using mobile banking applications were confirmed.
量尺：滿意：7-point semantic differential（四組雙極形容詞）；持續意圖與確認：7-point agree/disagree Likert
信度：Vedadi & Warkentin (2016) 複製研究（N = 256 大學生）：Satisfaction CR = .959、Continuance Intention .856、Confirmation .809；Bhattacherjee 2001 原始數值未核對（JSTOR 付費）
原始情境：原始研究為線上銀行（online banking services, OBS）使用者；本頁題項取自 Vedadi & Warkentin (2016, AIS Transactions on Replication Research, 2, Paper 7) 把 OBS 改成 mobile banking applications 的複製版
注意：原文題幹為 How do you feel about your overall experience of online banking use?，改編時只換系統名、四組形容詞不動。滿意四題改編自 Oliver (1980) 與 Spreng, MacKenzie & Olshavsky (1996) 的語意差異格式。持續意圖第三題是反向題，部分後續研究因負荷低刪除。

End-User Computing Satisfaction, EUCS (Doll & Torkzadeh 1988)

Doll, W. J., & Torkzadeh, G. (1988). The measurement of end-user computing satisfaction. MIS Quarterly, 12(2), 259–274. ↗

終端使用者對特定應用系統的滿意度，由內容、正確性、格式、易用性、及時性五個面向構成的二階構念。

  1. C1. Does the system provide the precise information you need?
  2. C2. Does the information content meet your needs?
  3. C3. Does the system provide reports that seem to be just about exactly what you need?
  4. C4. Does the system provide sufficient information?
  5. A1. Is the system accurate?
  6. A2. Are you satisfied with the accuracy of the system?
  7. F1. Do you think the output is presented in a useful format?
  8. F2. Is the information clear?
  9. E1. Is the system user friendly?
  10. E2. Is the system easy to use?
  11. T1. Do you get the information you need in time?
  12. T2. Does the system provide up-to-date information?
量尺：原版 5-point, almost never–almost always；Chin & Lee (2000) 改用 7-point: never / almost never / some of the time / about half of the time / most of the time / almost always / always
信度：原文整體 α 未在本次來源核對；Doll, Xia & Torkzadeh (1994) 以 409 人 CFA 支持五個一階因素＋一個二階因素；Torkzadeh & Doll (1991) 報告再測信度
原始情境：原始研究為 44 家公司 618 位終端使用者；題項取自 Chin & Lee (2000, ICIS Proceedings, Paper 57) Table 1 的逐字重製
注意：改編時把「the system」換成具體系統名（例：Does the CAS provide the precise information you need?）。Timeliness 兩題在網站情境常不穩定（Timeliness 需另行修訂）；Chin & Lee 另加整體滿意題 How would you rate your satisfaction with the use of the system?（very dissatisfied–very satisfied，-3 至 +3）可作總體滿意的單題。

ACSI customer satisfaction, 3-item index (Fornell et al. 1996)

Fornell, C., Johnson, M. D., Anderson, E. W., Cha, J., & Bryant, B. E. (1996). The American Customer Satisfaction Index: Nature, purpose, and findings. Journal of Marketing, 60(4), 7–18. ↗

顧客滿意為潛在變數，由整體滿意、期望落差（expectancy disconfirmation）、與理想比較三題在結構方程模型中加權而成。

  1. Using a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 means Very Dissatisfied and 10 means Very Satisfied, rate your overall satisfaction.
  2. Using a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 means Falls Short of Your Expectations and 10 means Exceeds Your Expectations, how well does your experience meet your expectations?
  3. Using a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 means Not Very Close to the Ideal and 10 means Very Close to the Ideal, how close was your experience to your "ideal" experience?
量尺：1–10；三題錨點分別為 Very dissatisfied–Very satisfied、Falls short of your expectations–Exceeds your expectations、Not very close to the ideal–Very close to the ideal
信度：ACSI 不報 α；三題權重由 PLS 結構模型估計並換算為 0–100 指數
原始情境：美國跨產業顧客滿意指數；題幹取自 CFI Group 為 NASA Planetary Data System 做的 ACSI 方法使用者調查（2020）
注意：題幹來源 https://pds.nasa.gov/home/NASAPDSUserSatisfaction2020final2.pdf （CFI Group 版本，另有以產品名代入的版本：how satisfied are you with PDS overall? / how does PDS compare to your expectations? / how does PDS compare to this ideal?）。Wikipedia 頁只列錨點。自行使用時常見簡化算法 (Satisfaction + Expectancy + Ideal − 3) / 27 × 100，與官方加權指數不同。

Satisfaction, expectancy-disconfirmation model (Oliver 1980)

Oliver, R. L. (1980). A cognitive model of the antecedents and consequences of satisfaction decisions. Journal of Marketing Research, 17(4), 460–469. ↗

滿意是期望與期望落差（disconfirmation）的函數，並影響後續態度與購買意圖。

來源未列出題項

量尺：原文為六題 Likert 格式整體滿意題（二手文獻描述），錨點未核對
信度：α = .82（二手文獻轉述 Oliver 1980 六題內部一致性）
原始情境：流感疫苗接種者與未接種者的兩階段現場研究
注意：原文在 SAGE 付費牆後（回 403），逐字題項未取得；本次只開到 Wikipedia 的理論頁與二手描述。後續多數研究並不直接沿用 Oliver 1980 原題，而是用 Oliver 的語意差異格式（Bhattacherjee 2001 四題即此類）或 Oliver (1997/2010) Satisfaction 專書中的量表。
