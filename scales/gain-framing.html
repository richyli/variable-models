Tversky & Kahneman (1981)

Tversky & Kahneman (1981) Science 211(4481), 453–458, Asian disease problem；分類見 Levin, Schneider & Gaeth (1998) attribute / goal / risky-choice framing ↗

  1. Gain frame (risky choice): If Program A is adopted, 200 people will be saved. If Program B is adopted, there is a 1/3 probability that 600 people will be saved and a 2/3 probability that no people will be saved.
  2. Loss frame (risky choice): If Program C is adopted, 400 people will die. If Program D is adopted, there is a 1/3 probability that nobody will die and a 2/3 probability that 600 people will die.
  3. Attribute framing: ground beef described as 75% lean (gain) vs. 25% fat (loss)
  4. Goal framing (health message): If you do X, you will gain benefit Y (gain) vs. If you do not do X, you will lose benefit Y (loss)
  5. Manipulation check: The message emphasized the benefits of acting (gain) / the costs of not acting (loss).
量尺：操弄變項（between-subjects）；依變項為選擇比例或 7 點態度／意圖
