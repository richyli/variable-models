Featherman & Pavlou (2003) 題項未對照原文

Featherman & Pavlou (2003) perceived risk facets（performance / financial / time / psychological / social / privacy / overall）；Jarvenpaa, Tractinsky & Vitale (2000) 整體風險 ↗

  1. How would you characterize the decision to use [e-service]? very negative situation – very positive situation (R)
  2. Using [e-service] would be risky
  3. There is a significant risk involved in using [e-service]
  4. Performance risk: [e-service] might not perform well and create problems with my [task]
  5. Financial risk: Using [e-service] would lead to a financial loss for me
  6. Privacy risk: Using [e-service] would cause me to lose control over the privacy of my information
量尺：7-point Likert (1=strongly disagree to 7=strongly agree) 或 7 點語意差異
信度：Featherman & Pavlou (2003) 各面向 α：time .796、psychological .891、privacy .857、financial .857、performance .797、social .814、overall .850（二手轉載，依摘要）
