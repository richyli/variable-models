Ajzen (1991, 2002/2006) TPB 直接測量（capacity / self-efficacy ＋ autonomy / controllability） ↗

  1. For me to [perform behavior X] would be: extremely difficult – extremely easy
  2. I am confident that I could [perform behavior X] if I wanted to
  3. Whether or not I [perform behavior X] is entirely up to me
  4. I have complete control over whether I [perform behavior X]
  5. There are factors outside my control that could prevent me from [performing behavior X] (R)
量尺：7-point bipolar 或 7-point Likert（strongly disagree – strongly agree）
信度：藥師前測 α = .422（刪一題後提高，依摘要）；Ajzen 指出 PBC 兩成分常使 α 偏低，實證多在 .60–.85
