Tornatzky & Fleischer (1990) 題項未對照原文

Tornatzky & Fleischer (1990) The Processes of Technological Innovation（TOE 的 technological context）；實證常用 Zhu, Kraemer & Xu (2006) technology competence / IT infrastructure 題 ↗

  1. Our firm has the IT infrastructure (hardware, network, databases) needed to support [the technology].
  2. Our firm has employees with the technical skills needed to implement and use [the technology].
  3. Our existing systems are compatible with [the technology].
  4. [The technology] is readily available in the market for our industry.
  5. Perceived relative advantage of [the technology] over existing practices.
量尺：7-point Likert（TOE 調查）；TTF 脈絡用 Goodhue & Thompson (1995) 的 technology characteristics 題
