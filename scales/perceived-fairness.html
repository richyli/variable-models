Colquitt (2001)

Colquitt (2001) Journal of Applied Psychology 86(3), 386–400, Organizational Justice Scale (procedural 7, distributive 4, interpersonal 4, informational 5) ↗

  1. To what extent have you been able to express your views and feelings during those procedures?（procedural）
  2. To what extent have those procedures been free of bias?（procedural）
  3. To what extent have those procedures been applied consistently?（procedural）
  4. To what extent have those procedures been based on accurate information?（procedural）
  5. To what extent has (he/she) treated you with respect?（interpersonal）
  6. To what extent has (he/she) refrained from improper remarks or comments?（interpersonal）
  7. To what extent has (he/she) explained the procedures thoroughly?（informational）
  8. To what extent were (his/her) explanations regarding the procedures reasonable?（informational）
量尺：5-point, 1 = to a small extent, 5 = to a large extent
信度：procedural α = .93, distributive .93, interpersonal .92, informational .90（Colquitt 2001 Study 1，依常見引用）

注意：前 2 題與後 4 題為頁面逐字；procedural 第 3、4 題依常見引用補。FAccT／演算法公平脈絡常改用 Shin (2020) 或 Lee (2018) 的 algorithmic fairness 3–4 題。
