Intrinsic Motivation Inventory（IMI）— Interest/Enjoyment 分量表

Ryan, R. M.（1982）。Control and information in the intrapersonal sphere: An extension of cognitive evaluation theory。Journal of Personality and Social Psychology，43，450–461；量表全文由 selfdeterminationtheory.org 發布；效度研究 McAuley, E., Duncan, T., & Tammen, V. V.（1989）。Psychometric properties of the Intrinsic Motivation Inventory in a competitive sport setting。Research Quarterly for Exercise and Sport，60，48–58 ↗

參與者對某一目標活動的主觀經驗；其中 interest/enjoyment 分量表被視為內在動機的自陳測量（the self-report measure of intrinsic motivation）。

  1. I enjoyed doing this activity very much
  2. This activity was fun to do.
  3. I thought this was a boring activity. (R)
  4. This activity did not hold my attention at all. (R)
  5. I would describe this activity as very interesting.
  6. I thought this activity was quite enjoyable.
  7. While I was doing this activity, I was thinking about how much I enjoyed it.
量尺：7-point（1 = not at all true, 4 = somewhat true, 7 = very true）
信度：官方文件未報 α；載明 McAuley, Duncan & Tammen（1989）CFA 支持效度，題項入選標準為因素負荷 ≥ .60 且交叉負荷 < .40
原始情境：實驗室情境下受試者完成某項活動（拼圖、閱讀教材等）後的事後測量
注意：全套 45 題共 7 個分量表（interest/enjoyment、perceived competence、effort/importance、pressure/tension、perceived choice、value/usefulness、relatedness），研究者依問題自選分量表並隨機排序；官方說明允許把「this activity」改成具體活動而不影響信效度。(R) 題反向計分（8 − 原分）。常用版本：22 題 Task Evaluation Questionnaire（interest/enjoyment 為第 1, 5, 8, 10, 14(R), 17, 20 題，另含 perceived competence、perceived choice、pressure/tension）；9 題閱讀版；25 題內化研究版。官方提醒自陳 interest 與行為指標相關僅約 .4，且每個因素超過 4 題後增量 R 很小。題項另見 https://psytests.org/sdt/imien-bl.html 。

Situational Motivation Scale（SIMS）

Guay, F., Vallerand, R. J., & Blanchard, C.（2000）。On the assessment of situational intrinsic and extrinsic motivation: The Situational Motivation Scale (SIMS)。Motivation and Emotion，24(3)，175–213 ↗

個人在特定時刻、特定情境下從事某活動的動機（motivation toward a particular activity at a specific moment or within a particular situation），區分內在動機、認同調節、外在調節與無動機。

  1. Why are you currently engaged in this activity? — Because I think that this activity is interesting.
  2. Because I am doing it for my own good.
  3. Because I am supposed to do it.
  4. There may be good reasons to do this activity, but personally I don't see any.
  5. Because I think that this activity is pleasant.
  6. Because I think that this activity is good for me.
  7. Because it is something that I have to do.
  8. I do this activity but I am not sure if it is worth it.
  9. Because this activity is fun.
  10. By personal decision.
  11. Because I don't have any choice.
  12. I don't know; I don't see what this activity brings me.
  13. Because I feel good when doing this activity.
  14. Because I believe that this activity is important for me.
  15. Because I feel that I have to do it.
  16. I do this activity, but I am not sure it is a good thing to pursue it.
量尺：7-point（1 = Corresponds not at all, 2 = Corresponds a very little, 3 = Corresponds a little, 4 = Corresponds moderately, 5 = Corresponds enough, 6 = Corresponds a lot, 7 = Corresponds exactly）
信度：原文各分量表 α 未在打開的來源取得（Springer 原文頁需登入）
原始情境：教育與體育活動情境；大學生樣本
注意：四個分量表各 4 題，依題意歸類：intrinsic motivation（1, 5, 9, 13）、identified regulation（2, 6, 10, 14）、external regulation（3, 7, 11, 15）、amotivation（4, 8, 12, 16）；此對應依原量表慣例，psytests 頁面本身未標示。Standage, Treasure, Duda & Prusak（2003, J Sport & Exercise Psychology）在體育情境刪 2 題成 14 題版並只得到部分恆等性；挪威、西班牙（12 題版）、葡萄牙、印尼驗證也多建議刪題。改編時「this activity」換成具體活動。

Perceived Enjoyment（內在動機）／Perceived Usefulness（外在動機）

Davis, F. D., Bagozzi, R. P., & Warshaw, P. R.（1992）。Extrinsic and intrinsic motivation to use computers in the workplace。Journal of Applied Social Psychology，22(14)，1111–1132 ↗

知覺享樂（perceived enjoyment）＝使用電腦系統這項活動本身被知覺為令人愉快的程度，與工具價值無關（the extent to which the activity of using a computer system is perceived to be personally enjoyable in its own right aside from the instrumental value of the technology）；知覺有用性＝外在動機。

  1. I find using online self-regulated learning to be enjoyable
  2. The actual process of using online self-regulated learning is pleasant
  3. I have fun using online self-regulated learning
量尺：7-point Likert（依 TAM 系列慣例；本次未從原文核對）
信度：原文信度未在打開的來源取得
原始情境：職場電腦使用：Study 1 文書處理軟體、Study 2 商業繪圖程式；有用性對使用意圖 β = .68／.79，享樂 β = .16／.15，兩者合計解釋意圖變異 62%／75%
注意：逐字題項未取得（Wiley 原文頁被擋）。定義句引自 Roca & Gagné（2008，Computers in Human Behavior， https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2008_RocaGagne_CHP.pdf ）轉述。常見改編版：Turel, Serenko & Giles（2011, MIS Quarterly）附錄以「Using eBay.com is enjoyable / pleasurable / fun / exciting / interesting」五題標註出自 Davis et al. 1992（此為搜尋結果摘要，未打開原附錄）。改編時注意：此量表是 TAM 脈絡的「享樂」單一面向，與 SDT 的內在動機概念不同；Venkatesh（2000）把 enjoyment 當成 ease of use 的前因，UTAUT2 改名 hedonic motivation。；補查：三題結構完整，但轉載研究已將原文的系統名稱置換為「online self-regulated learning」，原文應為「I find using [system] to be enjoyable」等句型，置換處以外逐字相同；轉載表格明確標註來源 Davis et al. (1992)。另一轉載（PMC12135465, GETAMEL）用「I find using the VR anatomy software enjoyable」「The actual process of using the VR anatomy software is pleasant」兩題，α = 0.98。JASP 原文全文未取得。

Work Preference Inventory（WPI）

Amabile, T. M., Hill, K. G., Hennessey, B. A., & Tighe, E. M.（1994）。The Work Preference Inventory: Assessing intrinsic and extrinsic motivational orientations。Journal of Personality and Social Psychology，66(5)，950–967 ↗

個人對工作的內在動機取向（因挑戰、好奇、樂趣、自我表達而工作）與外在動機取向（因報酬、評價、他人認可、他人指令而工作）的穩定傾向。

  1. I am not that concerned about what other people think of me.
  2. I prefer having someone set clear goals for me in my work.
  3. The more difficult the problem, the more I enjoy trying to solve it.
  4. I am keenly aware of the goals I have for getting good grades.
  5. I want my work to provide me with opportunities for increasing my knowledge and skills.
  6. To me, success means doing better than other people.
  7. I prefer to figure things out for myself.
  8. No matter what the outcome of a project, I am satisfied if I feel I gained a new experience.
  9. I enjoy relatively simple, straightforward tasks.
  10. I am keenly aware of the GPA goals I have for myself.
  11. Curiosity is the driving force behind much of what I do.
  12. I'm less concerned with what work I do than what I get for it.
  13. I enjoy tackling problems that are completely new to me.
  14. I prefer work I know I can do well over work that stretches my abilities.
  15. I'm concerned about how other people are going to react to my ideas.
  16. I seldom think about grades and awards.
  17. I'm more comfortable when I get to set my own goals.
  18. I believe there is no point in doing a good job if nobody else knows about it.
  19. I am strongly motivated by the grades I can earn.
  20. It is important for me to be able to do what I most enjoy.
  21. I prefer working on projects with clearly specified procedures.
  22. As long as I can do what I enjoy, I'm not that concerned about exactly what grades or awards I can earn.
  23. I enjoy doing work that is so absorbing that I forget about everything else.
  24. I am strongly motivated by the recognition I can earn from other people.
  25. I have to feel that I'm earning something for what I do.
  26. I enjoy trying to solve complex problems.
  27. It is important for me to have an outlet for self-expression.
  28. I want to find out how good I really can be at my work.
  29. I want other people to find out how good I really can be at my work.
  30. What matters most to me is enjoying what I do.
量尺：4-point（1 = never or almost never true of me, 4 = always or almost always true of me）
信度：原文 α 未在打開的來源取得
原始情境：美國大學生（本份為學生版，題項提到 grades／GPA）；另有工作成人版
注意：UNCW 課程網頁轉載的是大學生版（college student version）；原文另有成人版把 grades／GPA 改為 salary／promotion 等職場字眼，本次未取得成人版逐字題項。計分：Intrinsic＝3+5+7+8+9(R)+11+13+14(R)+17+20+23+26+27+28+30；Extrinsic＝1(R)+2+4+6+10+12+15+16(R)+18+19+21+22(R)+24+25+29；文件載男女平均分別約 45 與 39。Intrinsic 下再分 challenge／enjoyment，Extrinsic 下再分 outward／compensation 次量表（依原文；次量表題號本次未核對）。文件中含撇號的題項（12, 15, 17, 22, 25）因文字抽取略去撇號，此處依句意還原「I'm」。
