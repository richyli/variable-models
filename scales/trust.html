Mayer & Davis (1999) Trust / Ability–Benevolence–Integrity (ABI) scales

Mayer, R. C., & Davis, J. H. (1999). The effect of the performance appraisal system on trust for management: A field quasi-experiment. Journal of Applied Psychology, 84(1), 123–136. ↗

信任＝願意讓自己暴露在受信任者的行動之下（willingness to be vulnerable）；可信度由能力、善意、正直三因素構成（Mayer, Davis & Schoorman 1995）。

  1. [Ability] Top management is very capable of performing its job.
  2. [Ability] Top management is known to be successful at the things it tries to do.
  3. [Ability] Top management has much knowledge about the work that needs done.
  4. [Ability] I feel very confident about top management's skills.
  5. [Ability] Top management has specialized capabilities that can increase our performance.
  6. [Ability] Top management is well qualified.
  7. [Benevolence] Top management is very concerned about my welfare.
  8. [Benevolence] My needs and desires are very important to top management.
  9. [Benevolence] Top management would not knowingly do anything to hurt me.
  10. [Benevolence] Top management really looks out for what is important to me.
  11. [Benevolence] Top management will go out of its way to help me.
  12. [Integrity] Top management has a strong sense of justice.
  13. [Integrity] I never have to wonder whether top management will stick to its word.
  14. [Integrity] Top management tries hard to be fair in dealings with others.
  15. [Integrity] Top management's actions and behaviors are not very consistent. (reverse-scored)
  16. [Integrity] I like top management's values.
  17. [Integrity] Sound principles seem to guide top management's behavior.
  18. [Trust] If I had my way, I wouldn't let top management have any influence over issues that are important to me. (reverse-scored)
  19. [Trust] I would be willing to let top management have complete control over my future in this company.
  20. [Trust] I really wish I had a good way to keep an eye on top management. (reverse-scored)
  21. [Trust] I would be comfortable giving top management a task or problem which was critical to me, even if I could not monitor their actions.
  22. [Propensity] One should be very cautious with strangers.
  23. [Propensity] Most experts tell the truth about the limits of their knowledge.
  24. [Propensity] Most people can be counted on to do what they say they will do.
  25. [Propensity] These days, you must be alert or someone is likely to take advantage of you.
  26. [Propensity] Most salespeople are honest in describing their products.
  27. [Propensity] Most repair people will not overcharge people who are ignorant of their specialty.
  28. [Propensity] Most people answer public opinion polls honestly.
  29. [Propensity] Most adults are competent at their jobs.
量尺：5-point Likert: 1 Disagree strongly / 2 Disagree / 3 Neither agree nor disagree / 4 Agree / 5 Agree strongly
信度：Ability α = .85 (Wave 2) / .88 (Wave 3)；Benevolence .87 / .89；Integrity .82 / .88；Propensity .55 / .66；Trust .59 / .60。Schoorman, Mayer & Davis (1996) 原始較長版本（10/12/13/7/4 題）α = .93 / .95 / .96 / .71 / .82
原始情境：一家公司員工評價公司高階管理團隊，14 個月三波現場準實驗（績效考核制度變更前後）
注意：Trust 四題 α 偏低（.59–.60），作者歸因於題數少；後續研究常只用 ABI 三個可信度分量表、不用 Trust 四題。改編到 AI／機器人時（arXiv 2301.09011）把「top management」換成對象，Integrity 的 justice／fairness 題與「go out of its way」題因不適用被刪。跨文化研究指出 Ability／Benevolence 部分題項在集體主義樣本解讀不同。

Trust in Automation Scale, TIAS (Jian, Bisantz & Drury 2000)

Jian, J.-Y., Bisantz, A. M., & Drury, C. G. (2000). Foundations for an empirically determined scale of trust in automated systems. International Journal of Cognitive Ergonomics, 4(1), 53–71. ↗

人對自動化系統的信任與不信任，視為同一向度的兩極（由信任相關詞彙的群集分析產生 12 題）。

  1. The system is deceptive. (reverse-scored)
  2. The system behaves in an underhanded manner. (reverse-scored)
  3. I am suspicious of the system's intent, action, or output. (reverse-scored)
  4. I am wary of the system. (reverse-scored)
  5. The system's action will have a harmful or injurious outcome. (reverse-scored)
  6. I am confident in the system.
  7. The system provides security.
  8. The system has integrity.
  9. The system is dependable.
  10. The system is reliable.
  11. I can trust the system.
  12. I am familiar with the system.
量尺：7-point, 1 = Not at all, 7 = Extremely
信度：原始論文未報告 α；McGrath et al. (2025) 以 AI 助理為對象 12 題 α = .94（Study 1）、.95（Study 3）
原始情境：原始研究以問卷字詞評定建立量表，未在特定系統上驗證；McGrath et al. 2025 以 AI 助理為對象重新驗證
注意：題項取自 McGrath, Lack, Tisch & Duenser (2025, Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, 8, 1582880) Table 1 的重製，原始 DTIC 報告與期刊全文本次打不開。前五題為不信任題需反向計分；Spain et al. (2008) 發現信任／不信任兩個相關因素；Gutzwiller et al. (2019) 指出量表有正向偏誤；第 12 題（familiar）常被認為不是信任題而拿掉。短版 S-TIAS 三題：I am confident in the system / The system is reliable / I can trust the system（α = .97）。

Trusting beliefs & trusting intentions, e-commerce (McKnight, Choudhury & Kacmar 2002)

McKnight, D. H., Choudhury, V., & Kacmar, C. (2002). Developing and validating trust measures for e-commerce: An integrative typology. Information Systems Research, 13(3), 334–359. ↗

Trusting beliefs＝相信對方具備可被信任的屬性（competence、benevolence、integrity）；trusting intentions＝願意依賴對方（willingness to depend、subjective probability of depending）。另有 disposition to trust 與 institution-based trust，共 16 個子構念。

  1. [Trusting Beliefs – Benevolence (TBB)]
  2. I believe that LegalAdvice.com would act in my best interest.
  3. If I required help, LegalAdvice.com would do its best to help me.
  4. LegalAdvice.com is interested in my well-being, not just its own.
  5. [Trusting Beliefs – Integrity (TBI)]
  6. LegalAdvice.com is truthful in its dealings with me.
  7. I would characterize LegalAdvice.com as honest.
  8. LegalAdvice.com would keep its commitments.
  9. LegalAdvice.com is sincere and genuine.
  10. [Trusting Beliefs – Competence (TBC)]
  11. LegalAdvice.com is competent and effective in providing legal advice.
  12. LegalAdvice.com performs its role of giving legal advice very well.
  13. Overall, LegalAdvice.com is a capable and proficient Internet legal advice provider.
  14. In general, LegalAdvice.com is very knowledgeable about the law.
  15. [Trusting Intentions – Willingness to Depend (GN)]
  16. When an important legal issue or problem arises, I would feel comfortable depending on the information provided by LegalAdvice.com.
  17. I can always rely on LegalAdvice.com in a tough legal situation.
  18. I feel that I could count on LegalAdvice.com to help with a crucial legal problem.
  19. Faced with a difficult legal situation that required me to hire a lawyer (for a fee), I would use the firm backing LegalAdvice.com.
  20. If I had a challenging legal problem, I would want to use LegalAdvice.com again. (原列於 Subjective Probability of Depending—Follow Advice 第 1 題，作者註明後來改歸 Willingness to Depend)
  21. [Trusting Intentions – Subjective Probability of Depending: Follow Advice (FA)]
  22. I would feel comfortable acting on the landlord/tenant information given to me by LegalAdvice.com.
  23. I would not hesitate to use the landlord/tenant information LegalAdvice.com supplied me.
  24. I would confidently act on the legal advice I was given by LegalAdvice.com.
  25. I would feel secure in using the landlord/tenant information from LegalAdvice.com.
  26. Based on the advice I just read, I would serve notice, wait, go ahead and get the repair done, and then deduct the cost of the repair from my rent.
  27. [Trusting Intentions – Subjective Probability of Depending: Give Information (GI)]
  28. I would be willing to provide information like my name, address, and phone number to LegalAdvice.com.
  29. I would be willing to provide my social security number to LegalAdvice.com.
  30. I would be willing to share the specifics of my legal issue with LegalAdvice.com.
  31. [Trusting Intentions – Subjective Probability of Depending: Make Purchases (MP)]
  32. Faced with a difficult legal situation, I would be willing to pay to access information on the LegalAdvice.com Web site.
  33. I would be willing to provide credit card information on the LegalAdvice.com Web site.
  34. Given a tough legal issue, I would be willing to pay for a 30-minute phone consultation with a LegalAdvice.com lawyer.
量尺：原文為 7-point Likert（依 ideas.repec 摘要頁無法確認錨點）
信度：本次未取得全文，未確認數值
原始情境：以假想的法律諮詢網站（legal advice Web site）為刺激物的驗證研究
注意：題項未能逐字取得：Semantic Scholar 指向的開放取用檔 msu.edu/~mcknig26/Measures.pdf 已隨 MSU AFS 退役而失效，INFORMS、ResearchGate、Semantic Scholar 頁面皆回 403；只留摘要頁。改編時注意 competence／benevolence／integrity 三個 belief 各 3–4 題、trusting intentions 分兩個子構念，後續研究（如 McKnight et al. 2011 trust in technology）把 benevolence 改成 helpfulness、competence 改成 functionality。需要原題請由圖書館取得 ISR 全文附錄。；補查：題項取自作者自存於 MSU 的 ISR 全文 PDF（Wayback Machine 2011-12-07 快照）附錄 Measures，完整；附錄另含 Disposition to Trust、Institution-Based Trust、Site Quality 等題，未列入。原 msu.edu 網址現已失效（Incapsula 擋）。

Trust in online vendor (Gefen 2000; Gefen, Karahanna & Straub 2003)

Gefen, D. (2000). E-commerce: The role of familiarity and trust. Omega, 28(6), 725–737.；Gefen, D., Karahanna, E., & Straub, D. W. (2003). Trust and TAM in online shopping: An integrated model. MIS Quarterly, 27(1), 51–90. ↗

消費者相信線上賣家（研究中為 Amazon.com）誠實、可靠、關心顧客且可預測的信念；Gefen 2003 把它與 TAM 整合。

  1. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it is honest
  2. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it cares about customers
  3. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it is not opportunistic
  4. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it provides good service
  5. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it is predictable
  6. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it is trustworthy
  7. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it knows its market
量尺：7-point, strongly agree (1)–strongly disagree (7)（搜尋摘要所引 Gefen 2000 的描述，未在全文核對）
信度：Gefen 2000（搜尋摘要所引）：trust in the vendor α = .90、familiarity .91、disposition to trust .85、purchase intentions .78、inquiry intentions .91（N = 217）
原始情境：Gefen 2000：217 位潛在使用者，情境為在 Amazon.com 查詢與購買書籍；Gefen 2003：MBA 學生對 Amazon.com 的線上購書
注意：兩篇全文皆打不開（ScienceDirect／JSTOR 付費；terry.uga.edu 的 PDF 被 Cloudflare 擋、ResearchGate 與 academia.edu 回 403），題項未逐字取得，只留 Drexel esploro 與 ideas.repec 的書目頁（Gefen 2003 書目頁：https://researchdiscovery.drexel.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Trust-and-TAM-in-online-shopping/991019168361704721）。Gefen 2003 的 familiarity、disposition to trust、trust、purchase intention 題皆沿用 Gefen 2000；需要原題請由圖書館取得。；補查：題項取自第一作者 Gefen 本人 2020 年開放取用論文 Table 2，文中明寫 Use、Trust、Familiarity 量表「based on Gefen et al. (2003b)」（即 MISQ 2003）。共 7 題，未能比對 MISQ 原附錄是否即此 7 題或僅取其中數題；Gefen (2000) Omega 的 trust 題未取得。MISQ 原文 PDF（淡江課程網址）已 404。
