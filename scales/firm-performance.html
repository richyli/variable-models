Venkatraman & Ramanujam 1986 企業績效測量架構（分類基模，非題項量表）

Venkatraman, N., & Ramanujam, V.（1986）。Measurement of business performance in strategy research: A comparison of approaches. Academy of Management Review, 11(4), 801–814（本條引用其 1985 年 Illinois 工作論文版） ↗

企業績效是多維構念：最窄的「財務績效」（銷售成長、獲利、EPS 等）包在較寬的「營運績效」（市占、品質、新產品、行銷效能）之內，再外層是「組織效能」；測量途徑依「財務 vs 營運指標」×「初級 vs 次級資料」分成十類。

來源未列出題項

量尺：不適用（架構論文）；作者主張同時納入財務與營運指標、並用多重資料來源
信度：不適用
原始情境：策略管理實證研究的績效操作化回顧
注意：這篇是後續所有「主觀自評績效」量表的理論出處，自評量表設計時應交代涵蓋的是哪一層（財務／營運／效能）。工作論文版與 AMR 正式版內容一致，正式版頁碼 801–814。常被一起引用的是 Dess & Robinson (1984) 證明主觀自評績效與客觀財務數據顯著相關，用來合理化自評做法。

Delaney & Huselid 1996 知覺組織績效／知覺市場績效

Delaney, J. T., & Huselid, M. A.（1996）。The impact of human resource management practices on perceptions of organizational performance. Academy of Management Journal, 39(4), 949–969 ↗

受訪高階主管將本組織過去三年的表現與同業其他組織相比所做的主觀評價；組織績效面向涵蓋品質、創新、人才、顧客與員工關係，市場績效面向涵蓋行銷、銷售成長、獲利與市占。

  1. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Quality of products, services, or programs?
  2. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Development of new products, services, or programs?
  3. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Ability to attract essential employees?
  4. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Ability to retain essential employees?
  5. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Satisfaction of customers or clients?
  6. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Relations between management and other employees?
  7. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Relations among employees in general?
  8. Compared to other organizations that do the same kind of work, how would you compare the organization's performance over the last 3 years in terms of... Marketing?
  9. Compared to other organizations that do the same kind of work, how would you compare the organization's performance over the last 3 years in terms of... Growth in sales?
  10. Compared to other organizations that do the same kind of work, how would you compare the organization's performance over the last 3 years in terms of... Profitability?
  11. Compared to other organizations that do the same kind of work, how would you compare the organization's performance over the last 3 years in terms of... Market share?
量尺：4-point Likert：1 = worse … 4 = much better（原文 Table 1 標示 range 1–4）
信度：知覺組織績效 7 題 α = .85（N = 590）；知覺市場績效 4 題 α = .86（N = 373，僅上市公司）
原始情境：美國 1991 年 National Organizations Survey（GSS 特別模組），590 家營利與非營利組織，由人資或高階主管回答
注意：前 7 題與後 4 題是兩個分開的量表，不要合併；市場績效量表只適用營利組織。後續 Rasch 分析（二手引述）指出原兩因素結構不佳，三因素（internal／external／market）較合理。台灣研究改編時常把「past 3 years」改成「過去一年」，並改 5 點或 7 點；也常只取 Quality、New products、Customer satisfaction 三題加市場績效 4 題。本頁所列題幹依原文表格，What about… 與 in terms of… 為原文題幹的接續語。

Wiklund & Shepherd 2005 小企業主觀績效（相對競爭者的財務與成長指標）

Wiklund, J., & Shepherd, D.（2005）。Entrepreneurial orientation and small business performance: A configurational approach. Journal of Business Venturing, 20(1), 71–91 ↗

小企業績效以成長（銷售成長率、員工成長）與財務績效（毛利、獲利、現金流）兩類指標合併衡量，由企業主／CEO 相對於競爭者自評。

  1. sales growth rate
  2. employee growth
  3. gross margin
  4. profitability
  5. cash flow
量尺：原文：財務三項為 7-point、相對於競爭者（much less than our competitors … much more than our competitors）；成長兩項以兩個時間點的實際數字計算（依 Kraus et al. 2012 的複製研究描述，原文全文未能開啟）
信度：原文 α 未能核對；Kraus, Rigtering, Hughes & Hosman (2012, Review of Managerial Science) 荷蘭 164 家 SME 複製研究改用 5 點（1 = extremely bad performance … 5 = excellent performance）自評
原始情境：瑞典小企業（原文 n = 413，依複製研究所述），CEO 郵寄問卷，兩波資料
注意：只找到五個指標名稱，不是完整題幹（原文 JBV 全文與複製研究的 ResearchGate 頁都打不開）；指標名稱取自 Kraus et al. (2012) 第 4 節逐字。同作者 2003 年 SMJ 論文（Knowledge-based resources, entrepreneurial orientation, and the performance of SMEs, 24(13), 1307–1314）用同一套指標。改編時常見做法是列 8–10 個指標（加 market share、customer satisfaction、new product success）請受訪者相對競爭者或相對預期打分，但那些加項不是 Wiklund & Shepherd 的原設計，標示出處時要分清。
