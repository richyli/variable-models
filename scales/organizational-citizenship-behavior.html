Williams & Anderson (1991)

Williams & Anderson (1991) Journal of Management 17(3), OCBI (7) + OCBO (7)；替代主量表 Podsakoff, MacKenzie, Moorman & Fetter (1990) 24 題五構面 ↗

  1. Helps others who have been absent.（OCBI）
  2. Helps others who have heavy work loads.（OCBI）
  3. Goes out of way to help new employees.（OCBI）
  4. Takes time to listen to co-workers' problems and worries.（OCBI）
  5. Attendance at work is above the norm.（OCBO）
  6. Gives advance notice when unable to come to work.（OCBO）
  7. Conserves and protects organizational property.（OCBO）
  8. Adheres to informal rules devised to maintain order.（OCBO）
量尺：5-point Likert, 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree（主管、同儕或自評）
信度：OCBI α = .61–.88；OCBO α = .70–.75（跨研究）
