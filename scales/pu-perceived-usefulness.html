別名：Perceived Performance、PCV Usefulness、PU、Perceived Benefits、Perceived Benefit、PCVed Usefulness、Perceived Usefulness

變數群：有用性與效益　›　知覺有用性　用到它的理論：CogAbsFAccTHabit-ISCHMSAMISCMPCMECTSPTTAMTRI

Davis (1989) Perceived Usefulness 六題原版（TAM）

Davis, F. D.（1989）。Perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, and user acceptance of information technology。MIS Quarterly，13(3)，319–340。doi:10.2307/249008 ↗

個人相信使用某一系統會提升其工作績效的程度

  1. Using [this product] in my job would enable me to accomplish tasks more quickly.
  2. Using [this product] would improve my job performance.
  3. Using [this product] in my job would increase my productivity.
  4. Using [this product] would enhance my effectiveness on the job.
  5. Using [this product] would make it easier to do my job.
  6. I would find [this product] useful in my job.
量尺：7-point；Study 1 用 agree/disagree Likert，Study 2 用 likelihood（extremely likely – extremely unlikely，各點皆有標籤）
信度：α = .98（usefulness）、.94（ease of use）；兩個研究合計 152 位使用者、四套應用程式（摘要所報）
原始情境：組織內員工與 MBA 學生評估辦公室軟體（電子郵件、文書編輯、繪圖等）；題項中的 [this product] 原文為受測系統名稱（如 CHART-MASTER）
注意：measuringu.com 整理頁標註第 2、3、4、6 題是後續 TAM 延伸（TAM2／TAM3）沿用的四題；反向題在 Study 1 因降低信度被刪除。改編時把 [this product] 換成系統名稱、把 my job 換成情境（如 my studies、my daily life）即可，PMC 等改編版多依此做法。摘要出處另見 https://verdi.cs.ucl.ac.uk/constructDB/publications/davis-perceived-1989.html

TAM2 Perceived Usefulness 四題版（Venkatesh & Davis 2000）

Venkatesh, V., & Davis, F. D.（2000）。A theoretical extension of the technology acceptance model: Four longitudinal field studies。Management Science，46(2)，186–204。doi:10.1287/mnsc.46.2.186.11926 ↗

個人相信使用某一系統會提升其工作績效的程度（沿用 Davis 1989 定義）

  1. Using [this product] would improve my job performance.
  2. Using [this product] in my job would increase my productivity.
  3. Using [this product] would enhance my effectiveness on the job.
  4. I would find [this product] useful in my job.
量尺：7-point Likert（strongly disagree – strongly agree）
信度：原文信度未能從開放來源核對
原始情境：四個組織、四套系統（N = 156）的縱貫田野研究，含自願與強制使用情境，三個時點施測
注意：此處題項是 measuringu 頁面標註「後續 TAM 延伸沿用」的 Davis 四題原文；TAM2 附錄實際用現在式措辭（Using the system improves my performance in my job 等），本次無法從可開啟的來源逐字核對，引用時請以原文附錄為準。Springer 補充檔（https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs12525-022-00593-5/MediaObjects/12525_2022_593_MOESM1_ESM.pdf）將 Using the system in my job would increase my productivity 歸於 Moore & Benbasat (1991) 的 relative advantage、再由 Venkatesh et al. (2003) 收入。常見縮短版就是這四題，後續 UTAUT 又改成下列四題。

UTAUT Performance Expectancy 四題（Venkatesh et al. 2003）

Venkatesh, V., Morris, M. G., Davis, G. B., & Davis, F. D.（2003）。User acceptance of information technology: Toward a unified view。MIS Quarterly，27(3)，425–478。doi:10.2307/30036540 ↗

個人相信使用系統有助於其獲得工作績效增益的程度

  1. I would find the system useful in my job.
  2. Using the system enables me to accomplish tasks more quickly.
  3. Using the system increases my productivity.
  4. If I use the system, I will increase my chances of getting a raise.
量尺：7-point Likert（strongly disagree – strongly agree）
信度：Kaphzan 等（2022，Frontiers in Psychiatry，以色列精神科醫師對遠距精神醫療）五點版 α = .858；Revisiting UTAUT for the Age of AI（arXiv 2510.15142，改成 AI 版）α = .81
原始情境：四個組織導入新系統的員工（縱貫）；PE 題項整合自 Davis 的 PU、Moore & Benbasat 的 relative advantage、Compeau & Higgins 的 outcome expectations
注意：四題分別來自 PU（useful in my job）、relative advantage（accomplish tasks more quickly）、job-fit／PU（increase my productivity）與 outcome expectations（getting a raise）。第 4 題「加薪」在學生、自願使用或非職場情境常被刪掉或改寫（如 improve my grades），改編時注意。Europe PMC 全文可見題項與 α：https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC8977602/fullTextXML ；AI 改編版見 https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.15142 附錄。
