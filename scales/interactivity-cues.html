變數群：任務與科技特性　›　可供性線索　用到它的理論：MAINPSI-CASPT

McMillan & Hwang (2002) 題項未對照原文

McMillan & Hwang (2002), Journal of Advertising 31(3), 29–42, Measures of Perceived Interactivity（MPI, 18 題：direction of communication / user control / time）；Liu (2003) 12 題（active control / two-way communication / synchronicity）

  1. [Two-way] The site enables two-way communication.
  2. [Two-way] The site facilitates concurrent communication.
  3. [Control] I felt that I had a lot of control over my visiting experiences at this site.
  4. [Time] The site loads fast / responds quickly to my input.
  5. [Global] The site is interactive.
量尺：7-point Likert, strongly disagree – strongly agree
信度：MPI 三構面 α 約 .80–.90（未核對）；學習者–AI 互動版整體 α .95（不同工具，搜尋摘要）
