UTAUT Behavioral Intention (Venkatesh et al. 2003)

Venkatesh, V., Morris, M. G., Davis, G. B., & Davis, F. D. (2003). User acceptance of information technology: Toward a unified view. MIS Quarterly, 27(3), 425–478. ↗

使用者打算在未來一段期間內使用該系統的意圖；UTAUT 把它當成使用行為的直接前因。

  1. BI1: I intend to use the system in the next <n> months.
  2. BI2: I predict I would use the system in the next <n> months.
  3. BI3: I plan to use the system in the next <n> months.
量尺：7-point scale（論文只說「1 為負向端、7 為正向端」，所有構念皆用七點量表；後續研究多用 strongly disagree–strongly agree）
信度：ICR = .92 / .90 / .90（Study 1，T1／T2／T3，N = 215）；.89 / .88 / .90（Study 2 跨組織驗證）
原始情境：四個組織導入新資訊系統的員工，訓練後（T1）、導入後一個月（T2）、三個月（T3）三波施測；使用行為另以系統紀錄的使用時長測量
注意：原文 Table 16 標示此三題「adapted from Davis et al. (1989)」。<n> 由研究者填入期間（常見 6 個月）。改編時「the system」換成具體系統名；遠距精神醫療的改編版（PMC8977602）把 BI2 改成 I predict I will use…，α = .975。三題高度同質，常見縮短版只留 BI1＋BI3。

UTAUT2 Behavioral Intention (Venkatesh, Thong & Xu 2012)

Venkatesh, V., Thong, J. Y. L., & Xu, X. (2012). Consumer acceptance and use of information technology: Extending the unified theory of acceptance and use of technology. MIS Quarterly, 36(1), 157–178. ↗

消費者情境下，繼續使用某項科技的意圖（措辭已從「採用」改成「持續使用」）。

  1. BI1. I intend to continue using mobile Internet in the future.
  2. BI2. I will always try to use mobile Internet in my daily life.
  3. BI3. I plan to continue to use mobile Internet frequently.
量尺：7-point Likert, strongly disagree–strongly agree
信度：論文表 1 報告所有反映性量表 ICR ≥ .75、AVE > .70（N = 1,512）；BI 個別數值見原文 Table 1
原始情境：香港 1,512 位行動上網消費者，兩階段線上調查，第一波後四個月再收使用資料
注意：適合「已在使用、問要不要繼續用」的情境；研究初次採用時應改回 UTAUT 2003 版。BI2「always try to use」語意偏習慣，改編到低頻率行為時常被拿掉。使用行為用六種應用的使用頻率做成形成性指標（見 Behavior）。

TPB Intention (Ajzen; Fishbein & Ajzen 2010 questionnaire guide)

Ajzen, I. (n.d.). Constructing a theory of planned behavior questionnaire. University of Massachusetts Amherst（依 Fishbein, M., & Ajzen, I. (2010). Predicting and changing behavior: The reasoned action approach. Psychology Press 附錄）。 ↗

意圖是行為的直接前因；題項必須與行為準則（target, action, context, time）完全相容，並以自我指向的方式陳述。

  1. I intend to exercise for at least 20 minutes, three times per week for the next three months.
量尺：7-point bipolar adjective scale, likely (1)–unlikely (7)（指南建議每個構念 5–6 題，各以七點雙極形容詞量尺作答）
信度：指南未報告數值；要求前測後各構念內部一致性要高（高 α），並以 CFA 檢查區辨效度，必要時刪 1–2 題
原始情境：範例行為為術後病人未來三個月每週運動三次、每次 20 分鐘；屬問卷建構指南，不是單一實證研究
注意：指南只示範一題意圖題；實務上常見的另外幾種句型（I plan to…／I will try to…／I expect to…）在本指南正文沒有列出，改編時請自行依 TACT 原則改寫並保持與行為題同一時間框架。

TAM self-predicted future usage / behavioral expectation (Davis 1989, Study 2)

Davis, F. D. (1989). Perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, and user acceptance of information technology. MIS Quarterly, 13(3), 319–340. ↗

受測者對自己未來是否會經常使用某系統的自我預測（Davis 稱為 behavioral expectation，引 Warshaw & Davis 1985）。

  1. Assuming Pendraw would be available on my job, I predict that I will use it on a regular basis in the future.
量尺：兩個 7-point 量尺：一個 likely–unlikely，另一個反向極性 improbable–probable
信度：原文未對此題報告信度；與 perceived usefulness 的相關 r = .85（pooled, n = 80）
原始情境：40 位 MBA 學生在實驗室試用兩套繪圖軟體（Chart-Master、Pendraw）後作答
注意：Davis (1989) 本身沒有三題式 BI 量表；常被引用的「I intend to use…」三題出自 Davis, Bagozzi & Warshaw (1989, Management Science 35(8), 982–1003)，本次找不到可開啟的全文，題項未能逐字確認；UTAUT 2003 明寫其 BI 三題改編自該文。Davis 1989 的 PU／PEOU 在 Study 2 用 extremely likely–extremely unlikely 七點量尺。

TAM self-reported current usage (Davis 1989, Study 1)

Davis, F. D. (1989). Perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, and user acceptance of information technology. MIS Quarterly, 13(3), 319–340. ↗

使用者自陳目前使用某系統的頻率。

  1. Participants were asked to self-report their degree of current usage of electronic mail and XEDIT on six-position categorical scales with boxes labeled "Don't use at all," "Use less than once each week," "Use about once each week," "Use several times a week," "Use about once each day," and "Use several times each day."
量尺：六格類別量尺：Don't use at all / Use less than once each week / Use about once each week / Use several times a week / Use about once each day / Use several times each day
信度：單題，無信度；與 perceived usefulness r = .63、與 ease of use r = .45（pooled n = 184）
原始情境：IBM Canada 員工使用 PROFS 電子郵件（n = 109）與 XEDIT 編輯器（n = 75）
注意：是單一自陳頻率題，不是多題量表。常見改編：加一題「每週使用時數」或改成 7 點 never–several times a day。Davis, Bagozzi & Warshaw (1989) 另以 14 週後的實際使用衡量，本次未取得全文。

UTAUT Use Behavior — system-log duration (Venkatesh et al. 2003)

Venkatesh, V., Morris, M. G., Davis, G. B., & Davis, F. D. (2003). User acceptance of information technology: Toward a unified view. MIS Quarterly, 27(3), 425–478. ↗

實際使用行為，以系統紀錄的使用時長（duration of use）客觀測量。

  1. Actual usage behavior was measured as duration of use via system logs.
量尺：客觀指標（使用時長），非問卷題
信度：不適用（客觀紀錄）
原始情境：四個組織導入新系統後六個月內的系統紀錄；論文提到因網路可用性影響，使用量指標對環境敏感
注意：UTAUT 原版沒有自陳使用行為題；拿不到系統紀錄的研究通常改用 Davis 1989 的頻率題或 UTAUT2 的頻率索引。

UTAUT2 Use — frequency index across applications (Venkatesh, Thong & Xu 2012)

Venkatesh, V., Thong, J. Y. L., & Xu, X. (2012). Consumer acceptance and use of information technology: Extending the unified theory of acceptance and use of technology. MIS Quarterly, 36(1), 157–178. ↗

使用行為＝使用種類（variety）與頻率的形成性複合指標。

  1. Please choose your usage frequency for each of the following:
  2. a) SMS
  3. b) MMS
  4. c) Ringtone and logo download
  5. d) Java games
  6. e) Browse websites
  7. f) Mobile e-mail
量尺：7-point frequency scale, never–many times per day
信度：形成性指標，不報 α；六個指標的權重介於 .2 與 .40
原始情境：香港行動上網消費者，第一波調查後四個月再測使用
注意：改編時把六個應用換成研究情境的功能清單；論文自承行為錨定量尺可能有較高共同方法變異。

TPB self-reported behavior / past behavior (Ajzen questionnaire guide)

Ajzen, I. (n.d.). Constructing a theory of planned behavior questionnaire. University of Massachusetts Amherst（依 Fishbein & Ajzen 2010 附錄）。 ↗

行為以事後自陳是否在指定期間內做出與意圖題完全相同定義的行為來測量。

  1. In the past three months, I have exercised for at least 20 minutes, three times per week.
  2. Three months following administration of the questionnaire (or another period as defined by the behavioral criterion), the participants are recontacted and asked to report whether they had exercised for at least 20 min, three times per week for the past three months.
量尺：過去行為題：7-point false (1)–true (7)；追蹤行為題：依行為準則回報是否做到（指南未給固定格式）
信度：指南未報告
原始情境：問卷建構指南範例（術後病人運動行為）
注意：行為題與意圖題的 TACT 四要素必須一致；指南提醒範例中的過去行為發生在手術前，未必能預測未來行為。

Mayer & Davis (1999) Trust / Ability–Benevolence–Integrity (ABI) scales

Mayer, R. C., & Davis, J. H. (1999). The effect of the performance appraisal system on trust for management: A field quasi-experiment. Journal of Applied Psychology, 84(1), 123–136. ↗

信任＝願意讓自己暴露在受信任者的行動之下（willingness to be vulnerable）；可信度由能力、善意、正直三因素構成（Mayer, Davis & Schoorman 1995）。

  1. [Ability] Top management is very capable of performing its job.
  2. [Ability] Top management is known to be successful at the things it tries to do.
  3. [Ability] Top management has much knowledge about the work that needs done.
  4. [Ability] I feel very confident about top management's skills.
  5. [Ability] Top management has specialized capabilities that can increase our performance.
  6. [Ability] Top management is well qualified.
  7. [Benevolence] Top management is very concerned about my welfare.
  8. [Benevolence] My needs and desires are very important to top management.
  9. [Benevolence] Top management would not knowingly do anything to hurt me.
  10. [Benevolence] Top management really looks out for what is important to me.
  11. [Benevolence] Top management will go out of its way to help me.
  12. [Integrity] Top management has a strong sense of justice.
  13. [Integrity] I never have to wonder whether top management will stick to its word.
  14. [Integrity] Top management tries hard to be fair in dealings with others.
  15. [Integrity] Top management's actions and behaviors are not very consistent. (reverse-scored)
  16. [Integrity] I like top management's values.
  17. [Integrity] Sound principles seem to guide top management's behavior.
  18. [Trust] If I had my way, I wouldn't let top management have any influence over issues that are important to me. (reverse-scored)
  19. [Trust] I would be willing to let top management have complete control over my future in this company.
  20. [Trust] I really wish I had a good way to keep an eye on top management. (reverse-scored)
  21. [Trust] I would be comfortable giving top management a task or problem which was critical to me, even if I could not monitor their actions.
  22. [Propensity] One should be very cautious with strangers.
  23. [Propensity] Most experts tell the truth about the limits of their knowledge.
  24. [Propensity] Most people can be counted on to do what they say they will do.
  25. [Propensity] These days, you must be alert or someone is likely to take advantage of you.
  26. [Propensity] Most salespeople are honest in describing their products.
  27. [Propensity] Most repair people will not overcharge people who are ignorant of their specialty.
  28. [Propensity] Most people answer public opinion polls honestly.
  29. [Propensity] Most adults are competent at their jobs.
量尺：5-point Likert: 1 Disagree strongly / 2 Disagree / 3 Neither agree nor disagree / 4 Agree / 5 Agree strongly
信度：Ability α = .85 (Wave 2) / .88 (Wave 3)；Benevolence .87 / .89；Integrity .82 / .88；Propensity .55 / .66；Trust .59 / .60。Schoorman, Mayer & Davis (1996) 原始較長版本（10/12/13/7/4 題）α = .93 / .95 / .96 / .71 / .82
原始情境：一家公司員工評價公司高階管理團隊，14 個月三波現場準實驗（績效考核制度變更前後）
注意：Trust 四題 α 偏低（.59–.60），作者歸因於題數少；後續研究常只用 ABI 三個可信度分量表、不用 Trust 四題。改編到 AI／機器人時（arXiv 2301.09011）把「top management」換成對象，Integrity 的 justice／fairness 題與「go out of its way」題因不適用被刪。跨文化研究指出 Ability／Benevolence 部分題項在集體主義樣本解讀不同。

Trust in Automation Scale, TIAS (Jian, Bisantz & Drury 2000)

Jian, J.-Y., Bisantz, A. M., & Drury, C. G. (2000). Foundations for an empirically determined scale of trust in automated systems. International Journal of Cognitive Ergonomics, 4(1), 53–71. ↗

人對自動化系統的信任與不信任，視為同一向度的兩極（由信任相關詞彙的群集分析產生 12 題）。

  1. The system is deceptive. (reverse-scored)
  2. The system behaves in an underhanded manner. (reverse-scored)
  3. I am suspicious of the system's intent, action, or output. (reverse-scored)
  4. I am wary of the system. (reverse-scored)
  5. The system's action will have a harmful or injurious outcome. (reverse-scored)
  6. I am confident in the system.
  7. The system provides security.
  8. The system has integrity.
  9. The system is dependable.
  10. The system is reliable.
  11. I can trust the system.
  12. I am familiar with the system.
量尺：7-point, 1 = Not at all, 7 = Extremely
信度：原始論文未報告 α；McGrath et al. (2025) 以 AI 助理為對象 12 題 α = .94（Study 1）、.95（Study 3）
原始情境：原始研究以問卷字詞評定建立量表，未在特定系統上驗證；McGrath et al. 2025 以 AI 助理為對象重新驗證
注意：題項取自 McGrath, Lack, Tisch & Duenser (2025, Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, 8, 1582880) Table 1 的重製，原始 DTIC 報告與期刊全文本次打不開。前五題為不信任題需反向計分；Spain et al. (2008) 發現信任／不信任兩個相關因素；Gutzwiller et al. (2019) 指出量表有正向偏誤；第 12 題（familiar）常被認為不是信任題而拿掉。短版 S-TIAS 三題：I am confident in the system / The system is reliable / I can trust the system（α = .97）。

Trusting beliefs & trusting intentions, e-commerce (McKnight, Choudhury & Kacmar 2002)

McKnight, D. H., Choudhury, V., & Kacmar, C. (2002). Developing and validating trust measures for e-commerce: An integrative typology. Information Systems Research, 13(3), 334–359. ↗

Trusting beliefs＝相信對方具備可被信任的屬性（competence、benevolence、integrity）；trusting intentions＝願意依賴對方（willingness to depend、subjective probability of depending）。另有 disposition to trust 與 institution-based trust，共 16 個子構念。

  1. [Trusting Beliefs – Benevolence (TBB)]
  2. I believe that LegalAdvice.com would act in my best interest.
  3. If I required help, LegalAdvice.com would do its best to help me.
  4. LegalAdvice.com is interested in my well-being, not just its own.
  5. [Trusting Beliefs – Integrity (TBI)]
  6. LegalAdvice.com is truthful in its dealings with me.
  7. I would characterize LegalAdvice.com as honest.
  8. LegalAdvice.com would keep its commitments.
  9. LegalAdvice.com is sincere and genuine.
  10. [Trusting Beliefs – Competence (TBC)]
  11. LegalAdvice.com is competent and effective in providing legal advice.
  12. LegalAdvice.com performs its role of giving legal advice very well.
  13. Overall, LegalAdvice.com is a capable and proficient Internet legal advice provider.
  14. In general, LegalAdvice.com is very knowledgeable about the law.
  15. [Trusting Intentions – Willingness to Depend (GN)]
  16. When an important legal issue or problem arises, I would feel comfortable depending on the information provided by LegalAdvice.com.
  17. I can always rely on LegalAdvice.com in a tough legal situation.
  18. I feel that I could count on LegalAdvice.com to help with a crucial legal problem.
  19. Faced with a difficult legal situation that required me to hire a lawyer (for a fee), I would use the firm backing LegalAdvice.com.
  20. If I had a challenging legal problem, I would want to use LegalAdvice.com again. (原列於 Subjective Probability of Depending—Follow Advice 第 1 題，作者註明後來改歸 Willingness to Depend)
  21. [Trusting Intentions – Subjective Probability of Depending: Follow Advice (FA)]
  22. I would feel comfortable acting on the landlord/tenant information given to me by LegalAdvice.com.
  23. I would not hesitate to use the landlord/tenant information LegalAdvice.com supplied me.
  24. I would confidently act on the legal advice I was given by LegalAdvice.com.
  25. I would feel secure in using the landlord/tenant information from LegalAdvice.com.
  26. Based on the advice I just read, I would serve notice, wait, go ahead and get the repair done, and then deduct the cost of the repair from my rent.
  27. [Trusting Intentions – Subjective Probability of Depending: Give Information (GI)]
  28. I would be willing to provide information like my name, address, and phone number to LegalAdvice.com.
  29. I would be willing to provide my social security number to LegalAdvice.com.
  30. I would be willing to share the specifics of my legal issue with LegalAdvice.com.
  31. [Trusting Intentions – Subjective Probability of Depending: Make Purchases (MP)]
  32. Faced with a difficult legal situation, I would be willing to pay to access information on the LegalAdvice.com Web site.
  33. I would be willing to provide credit card information on the LegalAdvice.com Web site.
  34. Given a tough legal issue, I would be willing to pay for a 30-minute phone consultation with a LegalAdvice.com lawyer.
量尺：原文為 7-point Likert（依 ideas.repec 摘要頁無法確認錨點）
信度：本次未取得全文，未確認數值
原始情境：以假想的法律諮詢網站（legal advice Web site）為刺激物的驗證研究
注意：題項未能逐字取得：Semantic Scholar 指向的開放取用檔 msu.edu/~mcknig26/Measures.pdf 已隨 MSU AFS 退役而失效，INFORMS、ResearchGate、Semantic Scholar 頁面皆回 403；只留摘要頁。改編時注意 competence／benevolence／integrity 三個 belief 各 3–4 題、trusting intentions 分兩個子構念，後續研究（如 McKnight et al. 2011 trust in technology）把 benevolence 改成 helpfulness、competence 改成 functionality。需要原題請由圖書館取得 ISR 全文附錄。；補查：題項取自作者自存於 MSU 的 ISR 全文 PDF（Wayback Machine 2011-12-07 快照）附錄 Measures，完整；附錄另含 Disposition to Trust、Institution-Based Trust、Site Quality 等題，未列入。原 msu.edu 網址現已失效（Incapsula 擋）。

Trust in online vendor (Gefen 2000; Gefen, Karahanna & Straub 2003)

Gefen, D. (2000). E-commerce: The role of familiarity and trust. Omega, 28(6), 725–737.；Gefen, D., Karahanna, E., & Straub, D. W. (2003). Trust and TAM in online shopping: An integrated model. MIS Quarterly, 27(1), 51–90. ↗

消費者相信線上賣家（研究中為 Amazon.com）誠實、可靠、關心顧客且可預測的信念；Gefen 2003 把它與 TAM 整合。

  1. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it is honest
  2. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it cares about customers
  3. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it is not opportunistic
  4. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it provides good service
  5. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it is predictable
  6. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it is trustworthy
  7. Based on my experience with the online vendor in the past, I know it knows its market

信度：Gefen 2000（搜尋摘要所引）：trust in the vendor α = .90、familiarity .91、disposition to trust .85、purchase intentions .78、inquiry intentions .91（N = 217）
原始情境：Gefen 2000：217 位潛在使用者，情境為在 Amazon.com 查詢與購買書籍；Gefen 2003：MBA 學生對 Amazon.com 的線上購書
注意：兩篇全文皆打不開（ScienceDirect／JSTOR 付費；terry.uga.edu 的 PDF 被 Cloudflare 擋、ResearchGate 與 academia.edu 回 403），題項未逐字取得，只留 Drexel esploro 與 ideas.repec 的書目頁（Gefen 2003 書目頁：https://researchdiscovery.drexel.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Trust-and-TAM-in-online-shopping/991019168361704721）。Gefen 2003 的 familiarity、disposition to trust、trust、purchase intention 題皆沿用 Gefen 2000；需要原題請由圖書館取得。；補查：題項取自第一作者 Gefen 本人 2020 年開放取用論文 Table 2，文中明寫 Use、Trust、Familiarity 量表「based on Gefen et al. Willingness to buy (Dodds, Monroe & Grewal 1991)

Dodds, W. B., Monroe, K. B., & Grewal, D. (1991). Effects of price, brand, and store information on buyers' product evaluations. Journal of Marketing Research, 28(3), 307–319. ↗

在給定價格、品牌、商店資訊下，買者願意購買該產品的程度（willingness to buy）。

  1. If I were going to buy a bicycle, the probability of buying this model is
  2. The probability that I would consider buying this bicycle is
  3. At the price shown, I would consider buying the product
  4. The likelihood of purchasing this laptop is…
  5. The likelihood of ever using this laptop is…
  6. My desire to own this laptop is…
量尺：原文為 7-point 雙極量尺（very low–very high 類）；arXiv 2504.05359 的改編版用 5-point bipolar very low–very high；PMC8987512 的改編版用 7-point Likert strongly disagree–strongly agree

Paas, F. G. W. C.（1992）。Training strategies for attaining transfer of problem-solving skills in statistics: A cognitive-load approach. Journal of Educational Psychology, 84(4), 429–434 ↗

  1. Please choose the category (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9) that applies to you: In the lecture that just finished I invested

  1. The topic/topics covered in the activity was/were very complex.
  2. The activity covered formulas that I perceived as very complex.
  3. The activity covered concepts and definitions that I perceived as very complex.
  4. The instructions and/or explanations during the activity were very unclear.
  5. The instructions and/or explanations were, in terms of learning, very ineffective.
  6. The instructions and/or explanations were full of unclear language.
  7. The activity really enhanced my understanding of the topic(s) covered.
  8. The activity really enhanced my knowledge and understanding of statistics.
  9. The activity really enhanced my understanding of the formulas covered.
  10. The activity really enhanced my understanding of concepts and definitions.
量尺：11-point：0 (not at all the case) 到 10 (completely the case)；指導語：All of the following questions refer to the activity (lecture, class, discussion session, skills training or study session) that just finished.
信度：α = .81（IL，題 1–3）、.75（EL，題 4–6）、.82（GL，題 7–10）；Study I 為 56 位心理與健康科學博士生統計課；CFA 另以三班大學部統計課（n = 171、136、148）確認

NASA-TLX（Task Load Index）

Hart, S. G., & Staveland, L. E.（1988）。Development of NASA-TLX (Task Load Index): Results of empirical and theoretical research. In P. A. Hancock & N. Meshkati (Eds.), Human mental workload (Advances in Psychology, Vol. 52, pp. 139–183). Amsterdam: North-Holland ↗

主觀工作負荷（workload）：由心智需求、體力需求、時間需求、自評表現、努力、挫折六個子量表加權平均而得的整體負荷分數。

  1. MENTAL DEMAND (Low/High): How much mental and perceptual activity was required (e.g., thinking, deciding, calculating, remembering, looking, searching, etc.)? Was the task easy or demanding, simple or complex, exacting or forgiving?
  2. PHYSICAL DEMAND (Low/High): How much physical activity was required (e.g., pushing, pulling, turning, controlling, activating, etc.)? Was the task easy or demanding, slow or brisk, slack or strenuous, restful or laborious?
  3. TEMPORAL DEMAND (Low/High): How much time pressure did you feel due to the rate or pace at which the tasks or task elements occurred? Was the pace slow and leisurely or rapid and frantic?
  4. EFFORT (Low/High): How hard did you have to work (mentally and physically) to accomplish your level of performance?
  5. PERFORMANCE (Good/Poor): How successful do you think you were in accomplishing the goals of the task set by the experimenter (or yourself)? How satisfied were you with your performance in accomplishing these goals?
  6. FRUSTRATION LEVEL (Low/High): How insecure, discouraged, irritated, stressed and annoyed versus secure, gratified, content, relaxed and complacent did you feel during the task?
量尺：每個子量表為 0–100 的視覺類比線（21 格、每格 5 分），兩端錨點 Low/High（Performance 為 Good/Poor）；原版另做 15 組兩兩比較算權重，加權後得 0–100 總分
信度：原文件未報 α（各子量表為單題）；Hart (2006) 回顧指出 Raw TLX（不加權）與加權版高度相關
原始情境：NASA Ames 航空／模擬操作任務的工作負荷評估，後廣泛用於人因、醫療、HCI
注意：以上題目文字取自 NASA 技術備忘錄 NASA-TLX v1.0 使用手冊 Appendix A 的子量表定義（OCR 文字已校正標點）。紙本評定表常見簡化問法 How mentally demanding was the task? 等，屬後來的紙本版。常見改編：Raw TLX（RTLX，省略配對加權）、只取 Mental Demand 與 Effort 當認知負荷指標（教育研究常見）、把 Physical Demand 拿掉。Performance 子量表方向與其他相反（Good 在左），分析前要注意是否反向。Wikipedia 條目 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA-TLX 另有整理。

Williams & Anderson 1991 角色內行為（IRB）＋組織公民行為（OCBI／OCBO）

Williams, L. J., & Anderson, S. E.（1991）。Job satisfaction and organizational commitment as predictors of organizational citizenship and in-role behaviors. Journal of Management, 17(3), 601–617 ↗

角色內行為（in-role behavior）指正式工作說明書要求、且為獎酬制度所認可的行為；OCBI 為對特定個人有益的公民行為，OCBO 為對組織整體有益的公民行為。

  1. Adequately completes assigned duties.
  2. Fulfills responsibilities specified in job description.
  3. Performs tasks that are expected of him/her.
  4. Meets formal performance requirements of the job.
  5. Engages in activities that will directly affect his/her performance evaluation.
  6. Neglects aspects of the job he/she is obligated to perform.
  7. Fails to perform essential duties.
  8. Helps others who have been absent.
  9. Helps others who have heavy work loads.
  10. Assists supervisor with his/her work (when not asked).
  11. Takes time to listen to co-workers' problems and worries.
  12. Goes out of way to help new employees.
  13. Takes a personal interest in other employees.
  14. Passes along information to co-workers.
  15. Attendance at work is above the norm.
  16. Gives advance notice when unable to come to work.
  17. Takes undeserved work breaks.
  18. Great deal of time spent with personal phone conversations.
  19. Complains about insignificant things at work.
  20. Conserves and protects organizational property.
  21. Adheres to informal rules devised to maintain order.
量尺：5-point Likert（1 = Strongly disagree … 5 = Strongly agree；整理頁所載，原文由主管評）
信度：整理頁彙整：IRB α = .80–.94、OCBI α = .61–.88、OCBO α = .70–.75；原文 127 位主管評部屬
原始情境：美國企業員工，由直屬主管評定；原始設計為主管他評，後續多改為自評
注意：題項取自二手整理頁（scales.arabpsychology.com），順序為 IRB 7 題（含 2 題反向：Neglects…、Fails…）、OCBI 7 題、OCBO 7 題（含 3 題反向：Takes undeserved work breaks、Great deal of time…、Complains…）。後續 IRT 研究指出 OCBO 的反向題實際上測的是反生產工作行為（CWB）而非 OCB，許多研究因此拿掉這 3 題；短版常只用 IRB 前 4–5 題正向題。自評時「him/her」改「I」。本量表是「工作績效」研究中最常用的角色內績效自評／他評工具。

Goodman & Svyantek 1999 任務績效與脈絡績效量表

Goodman, S. A., & Svyantek, D. J.（1999）。Person–organization fit and contextual performance: Do shared values matter. Journal of Vocational Behavior, 55(2), 254–275 ↗

任務績效（task performance）為職務核心技術工作的熟練程度；脈絡績效（contextual performance）為支持組織社會與心理環境的自發行為，含利他（altruism）與盡責（conscientiousness）兩子面向。

  1. Helps other employees with their work when they have been absent.
  2. Takes initiative to orient new employees to the department even though it is not a part of his/her job description.
  3. Achieves the objectives of the job.
  4. Performs well in the overall job by carrying out tasks as expected.
量尺：7-point Likert（原版；Bağcı & Ünal 2019 土耳其版改 6 點 1 = Strongly Disagree … 6 = Strongly Agree）
信度：原文 α 未能核對；Bağcı & Ünal (2019) 486 位公立醫院員工與其主管自評／他評，MTMM 支持兩因素區辨效度
原始情境：美國企業員工，由同事或主管評；後續常用於自評＋主管評配對設計
注意：只找到 4 題（europeanproceedings 的 MTMM 研究引述的例題），原量表共 25 題：前 16 題為脈絡績效（利他 7 題＋盡責 9 題），後 9 題為任務績效。原文全文（Elsevier）及重印全題項的論文庫均打不開（403），上線前要補齊。常見縮短版只用 altruism 7 題＋task 9 題（共 16 題），盡責面向因與 OCBO 重疊常被拿掉。；補查：不完整：原量表 25 題（前 16 題 contextual、後 9 題 task，見 https://www.europeanproceedings.com/article/10.15405/epsbs.2019.12.03.29 ）；轉載研究 PMC9518503 附錄 Table A1 只列 9 題 task 與 7 題 contextual（利他／altruism 子構面），contextual 的另 9 題（conscientiousness 子構面）未在任何開放來源找到。轉載版本可能有輕微編修（如「Meet criteria」），無法與原文核對。

Individual Work Performance Questionnaire（IWPQ 1.0，Koopmans 2014）

Koopmans, L., Bernaards, C. M., Hildebrandt, V. H., de Vet, H. C. W., & van der Beek, A. J.（2014）。Construct validity of the Individual Work Performance Questionnaire. Journal of Occupational and Environmental Medicine, 56(3), 331–337；美式英文版見 Koopmans, L.（2015）。Individual Work Performance Questionnaire instruction manual. Amsterdam: TNO Innovation for Life – VU University Medical Center ↗

個人工作績效為「與組織目標相關的行為或行動」（Campbell, 1990），分任務績效、脈絡績效、反生產工作行為三個面向，只測行為不測結果。

  1. I was able to plan my work so that I finished it on time.
  2. I kept in mind the work result I needed to achieve.
  3. I was able to set priorities.
  4. I was able to carry out my work efficiently.
  5. I managed my time well.
  6. On my own initiative, I started new tasks when my old tasks were completed.
  7. I took on challenging tasks when they were available.
  8. I worked on keeping my job-related knowledge up-to-date.
  9. I worked on keeping my work skills up-to-date.
  10. I came up with creative solutions for new problems.
  11. I took on extra responsibilities.
  12. I continually sought new challenges in my work.
  13. I actively participated in meetings and/or consultations.
  14. I complained about minor work-related issues at work.
  15. I made problems at work bigger than they were.
  16. I focused on the negative aspects of situation at work instead of the positive aspects.
  17. I talked to colleagues about the negative aspects of my work.
  18. I talked to people outside the organization about the negative aspects of my work.
量尺：5-point，回溯期 past 3 months；任務與脈絡績效：0 = Seldom、1 = Sometimes、2 = Regularly、3 = Often、4 = Always；反生產工作行為：0 = Never、1 = Seldom、2 = Sometimes、3 = Regularly、4 = Often
信度：荷蘭版（n = 1,424）：任務績效 PSI .81／α .78、脈絡績效 PSI .85、CWB PSI .74（手冊 Table 1）；美式英文版 α = .79、.83、.89（三個量表依序）；西班牙版 α = .83、.87、.77（Ramos-Villagrasa et al. 2019）
原始情境：荷蘭各行業員工（藍領、白領、粉領皆有），自評；美式英文版經跨文化調適後在美國工作者驗證
注意：題項為 TNO 手冊第 7 節 American-English version 逐字；荷蘭原版英譯（Koopmans 2014 博士論文）措辭略不同（例：I managed to plan my work so that it was done on time / I complained about unimportant matters at work），引用時要註明用的是哪個版本。原 47 題版還有「適應性績效」面向，1.0 版已併入脈絡績效。三個量表分開計分（各取平均 0–4），不建議加總成單一總分。學術用途免費，商業用途須向 TNO/VUmc 取得授權。

Computer Self-Efficacy（CSE；Compeau & Higgins 1995）

Compeau, D. R., & Higgins, C. A.（1995）。Computer self-efficacy: Development of a measure and initial test. MIS Quarterly, 19(2), 189–211 ↗

個人對自己有能力使用電腦完成工作的判斷（不是指某個特定軟體技能，而是對完成整體任務的能力信念）。

  1. I could complete the job using the software package… if there was no one around to tell me what to do as I go.
  2. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had never used a package like it before.
  3. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had only the software manuals for reference.
  4. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had seen someone else using it before trying it myself.
  5. I could complete the job using the software package… if I could call someone for help if I got stuck.
  6. I could complete the job using the software package… if someone else had helped me get started.
  7. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had a lot of time to complete the job for which the software was provided.
  8. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had just the built-in help facility for assistance.
  9. I could complete the job using the software package… if someone showed me how to do it first.
  10. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had used similar packages before this one to do the same job.
量尺：兩段式：先答 Yes/No（能不能做到），答 Yes 再評信心 1 = Not at all confident、5 = Moderately confident、10 = Totally confident（10 點）
信度：α = .80（Learning Lab 整理頁所載）；Bao et al. (2013) 中國行動學習樣本 α = .911
原始情境：加拿大知識工作者（原文 n ≈ 1,020 位 Financial Post 500 企業經理人與專業人員郵寄問卷）
注意：題項對照 Learning Lab 附件 PDF 與 Bao, Xiong, Hu & Kibelloh (2013, Journal of Educational Computing Research 49(1), 111–132；預印本 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.4211 ）附錄，兩者一致（附件第 4 題漏印 trying，此處依 Bao 版）。Marakas 等人批評此量表測的是「一般 CSE」，與特定任務的「特定 CSE」要分開；研究手機 App 或 AI 工具時通常自編特定 CSE 題（I believe I have the ability to …）。多數後續研究省略 Yes/No 那一段，直接用 10 點信心評分。題幹 software package 可依情境改成具體系統名。

General Self-Efficacy Scale（GSE；Schwarzer & Jerusalem 1995）

Schwarzer, R., & Jerusalem, M.（1995）。Generalized Self-Efficacy scale. In J. Weinman, S. Wright, & M. Johnston (Eds.), Measures in health psychology: A user's portfolio. Causal and control beliefs (pp. 35–37). Windsor, UK: NFER-NELSON ↗

一般性自我效能：相信自己能應付各領域中新的或困難的任務、能因應逆境的樂觀自我信念。

  1. I can always manage to solve difficult problems if I try hard enough.
  2. If someone opposes me, I can find the means and ways to get what I want.
  3. It is easy for me to stick to my aims and accomplish my goals.
  4. I am confident that I could deal efficiently with unexpected events.
  5. Thanks to my resourcefulness, I know how to handle unforeseen situations.
  6. I can solve most problems if I invest the necessary effort.
  7. I can remain calm when facing difficulties because I can rely on my coping abilities.
  8. When I am confronted with a problem, I can usually find several solutions.
  9. If I am in trouble, I can usually think of a solution.
  10. I can usually handle whatever comes my way.
量尺：4-point：1 = Not at all true、2 = Hardly true、3 = Moderately true、4 = Exactly true；加總 10–40，無反向題
信度：作者網頁：23 國樣本 α = .76–.90，多數在 .80 後段；德國五個樣本 α = .82–.93；Scholz et al. (2002) 25 國 n = 19,120 國際平均 29.55
原始情境：一般成人與青少年；原為 1979 年德文 20 題版，1981 年縮為 10 題，英文版 1995 年出版；已有 30 餘種語言版（含中文）
注意：題項取自作者 Ralf Schwarzer 的 FU Berlin 官方頁；PsyToolkit 版（ https://psytoolkit.org/survey-library/generalized-self-efficacy-gse.html ）第 3、5、8、9、10 題措辭略有出入（少了 usually 等字），引用時以作者頁為準。非商業研究免申請授權，但要註明出處。有些研究改成 5 點或 7 點，與常模比較時要換算。中文版已由 Zhang & Schwarzer (1995) 發表。

New General Self-Efficacy Scale（NGSE；Chen, Gully & Eden 2001）

Chen, G., Gully, S. M., & Eden, D.（2001）。Validation of a new general self-efficacy scale. Organizational Research Methods, 4(1), 62–83 ↗

個人對自己在各種情境中達成表現的整體能力信念（one's belief in one's overall competence to effect requisite performances across a wide variety of achievement situations）。

  1. I will be able to achieve most of the goals that I have set for myself.
  2. When facing difficult tasks, I am certain that I will accomplish them.
  3. In general, I think that I can obtain outcomes that are important to me.
  4. I believe I can succeed at most any endeavor to which I set my mind.
  5. I will be able to successfully overcome many challenges.
  6. I am confident that I can perform effectively on many different tasks.
  7. Compared to other people, I can do most tasks very well.
  8. Even when things are tough, I can perform quite well.
量尺：5-point Likert：1 = Strongly disagree … 5 = Strongly agree；取平均
信度：二手整理頁報 α = .85–.90（scales.arabpsychology.com）；原文兩國（美國、以色列）多個樣本信度高、且比 Sherer 等人的 SGSE 更單向度，但原文確切 α 無法從可開啟頁面核對
原始情境：美國與以色列大學生與軍人（管理／組織心理學情境），用來預測各種任務的特定自我效能
注意：題項對照 SPARQ 與 UCSD EMERGE 兩頁一致（EMERGE 第 1 題為 that I have set、第 4 題為 almost any；SPARQ 第 1 題 that I set、第 4 題 most any——以原文為準者建議採 EMERGE 版）。與 GSE 的差別：NGSE 由 14 題池篩出 8 題、單因素、題目全為「能達成目標／克服挑戰」的 can-do 語氣；GSE 混有因應（coping）成分。組織行為研究偏用 NGSE，健康心理學偏用 GSE。

Bandura 自我效能量表編製指引（領域專屬量表的通用格式）

Bandura, A.（2006）。Guide for constructing self-efficacy scales. In F. Pajares & T. Urdan (Eds.), Self-efficacy beliefs of adolescents (Vol. 5, pp. 307–337). Greenwich, CT: Information Age Publishing ↗

自我效能是對自己「能夠」（can do）組織並執行達成特定成果所需行動的能力判斷，必須針對特定功能領域量身編題，不存在通用題項。

  1. The items should be phrased in terms of can do rather than will do. Can is a judgment of capability; will is a statement of intention.（Bandura 2006, p. 308）
  2. Participants record the strength of their efficacy beliefs on a 100-point scale, ranging in 10-unit intervals from 0 (Cannot do); through intermediate degrees of assurance, 50 (Moderately certain can do); to complete assurance, 100 (Highly certain can do).（Bandura 2006, p. 312）
量尺：0–100、每 10 分一格（0 = Cannot do；50 = Moderately certain can do；100 = Highly certain can do）；亦可用 0–10 的 11 點版，但 Bandura 反對 5 點或 7 點（區辨力不足）
信度：不適用（指引非固定量表）
原始情境：方法指引；範例量表（學業、自我調節學習、社交等）附於該章
注意：本條不是量表，而是編題規則；Bandura 原章 PDF 各鏡像（uky.edu、emory.edu）已失效，引文取自 Reading Matrix 期刊文章（Wyatt 2021 評述）逐字引用的頁碼。編題要點：題目列出不同難度等級的任務（graded difficulty）、避免「will」、避免天花板效應、先讓受試者練習評分、量表要與結果變項的領域對齊。學生常見錯誤是拿 GSE 這類一般量表去預測特定行為，Bandura 明確反對。

Venkatraman, N., & Ramanujam, V.（1986）。Measurement of business performance in strategy research: A comparison of approaches. Academy of Management Review, 11(4), 801–814（本條引用其 1985 年 Illinois 工作論文版） ↗

來源未列出題項

量尺：不適用（架構論文）；作者主張同時納入財務與營運指標、並用多重資料來源
信度：不適用
原始情境：策略管理實證研究的績效操作化回顧
注意：這篇是後續所有「主觀自評績效」量表的理論出處，自評量表設計時應交代涵蓋的是哪一層（財務／營運／效能）。工作論文版與 AMR 正式版內容一致，正式版頁碼 801–814。常被一起引用的是 Dess & Robinson (1984) 證明主觀自評績效與客觀財務數據顯著相關，用來合理化自評做法。

Delaney & Huselid 1996 知覺組織績效／知覺市場績效

Delaney, J. T., & Huselid, M. A.（1996）。The impact of human resource management practices on perceptions of organizational performance. Academy of Management Journal, 39(4), 949–969 ↗

受訪高階主管將本組織過去三年的表現與同業其他組織相比所做的主觀評價；組織績效面向涵蓋品質、創新、人才、顧客與員工關係，市場績效面向涵蓋行銷、銷售成長、獲利與市占。

  1. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Quality of products, services, or programs?
  2. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Development of new products, services, or programs?
  3. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Ability to attract essential employees?
  4. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Ability to retain essential employees?
  5. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Satisfaction of customers or clients?
  6. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Relations between management and other employees?
  7. How would you compare the organization's performance over the past 3 years to that of other organizations that do the same kind of work? What about... Relations among employees in general?
  8. Compared to other organizations that do the same kind of work, how would you compare the organization's performance over the last 3 years in terms of... Marketing?
  9. Compared to other organizations that do the same kind of work, how would you compare the organization's performance over the last 3 years in terms of... Growth in sales?
  10. Compared to other organizations that do the same kind of work, how would you compare the organization's performance over the last 3 years in terms of... Profitability?
  11. Compared to other organizations that do the same kind of work, how would you compare the organization's performance over the last 3 years in terms of... Market share?
量尺：4-point Likert：1 = worse … 4 = much better（原文 Table 1 標示 range 1–4）
信度：知覺組織績效 7 題 α = .85（N = 590）；知覺市場績效 4 題 α = .86（N = 373，僅上市公司）
原始情境：美國 1991 年 National Organizations Survey（GSS 特別模組），590 家營利與非營利組織，由人資或高階主管回答
注意：前 7 題與後 4 題是兩個分開的量表，不要合併；市場績效量表只適用營利組織。後續 Rasch 分析（二手引述）指出原兩因素結構不佳，三因素（internal／external／market）較合理。台灣研究改編時常把「past 3 years」改成「過去一年」，並改 5 點或 7 點；也常只取 Quality、New products、Customer satisfaction 三題加市場績效 4 題。本頁所列題幹依原文表格，What about… 與 in terms of… 為原文題幹的接續語。

Wiklund & Shepherd 2005 小企業主觀績效（相對競爭者的財務與成長指標）

Wiklund, J., & Shepherd, D.（2005）。Entrepreneurial orientation and small business performance: A configurational approach. Journal of Business Venturing, 20(1), 71–91 ↗

小企業績效以成長（銷售成長率、員工成長）與財務績效（毛利、獲利、現金流）兩類指標合併衡量，由企業主／CEO 相對於競爭者自評。

  1. sales growth rate
  2. employee growth
  3. gross margin
  4. profitability
  5. cash flow
量尺：原文：財務三項為 7-point、相對於競爭者（much less than our competitors … much more than our competitors）；成長兩項以兩個時間點的實際數字計算（依 Kraus et al. 2012 的複製研究描述，原文全文未能開啟）
信度：原文 α 未能核對；Kraus, Rigtering, Hughes & Hosman (2012, Review of Managerial Science) 荷蘭 164 家 SME 複製研究改用 5 點（1 = extremely bad performance … 5 = excellent performance）自評
原始情境：瑞典小企業（原文 n = 413，依複製研究所述），CEO 郵寄問卷，兩波資料

Dess & Beard (1984) 環境面向的檔案指標：Dynamism／Munificence／Complexity

Dess, G. G., & Beard, D. W.（1984）。Dimensions of organizational task environments。Administrative Science Quarterly，29(1)，52–73。doi:10.2307/2393080 ↗

任務環境以產業層級的客觀統計描述：動態性（不穩定）、豐饒度（成長）、複雜度（集中）

  1. Dynamism（instability）：產業出貨值（value of shipments）、價格—成本邊際、總就業人數、附加價值等序列的離散程度——標準差（或原文：以時間為自變項迴歸該序列後，迴歸斜率的標準誤）除以該序列平均值
  2. Munificence（growth）：同一組序列對時間迴歸的斜率係數除以序列平均值；另加事業單位數的年均成長率
  3. Complexity（concentration）：產業銷售、附加價值、就業人數、事業單位數在各人口普查分區的地理集中度（各分區值平方和除以總值平方）
量尺：客觀指標（產業層級；以美國製造業普查資料計算，通常取 5 年窗）
信度：不適用；Porto 等（2008，BASE Revista de Administração e Contabilidade da Unisinos）以 1997–2002 美國 466 個製造業部門複製，因素結構獲得支持
原始情境：美國製造業四位數 SIC 產業，1968–1977 普查資料
注意：操作化清單取自 Porto, Brito, Silva & Bataglia 的複製研究 Quadro 1（Adaptado de Dess e Beard 1984）；該表的 dynamism 用「標準差／平均」，Dess & Beard 原文與 Keats & Hitt、Misangyi 等的慣用做法是「對時間迴歸的斜率標準誤／平均」（去趨勢後的波動），兩者不同，引用時要說明用哪一種。Sharfman & Dean (1991) 批評此法遺漏若干理論面向並提出修訂指標。

Grit Scale（Grit-O）Perseverance of Effort 子量表（Duckworth et al. 2007）

Duckworth, A. L., Peterson, C., Matthews, M. D., & Kelly, D. R.（2007）。Grit: Perseverance and passion for long-term goals。Journal of Personality and Social Psychology，92(6)，1087–1101。doi:10.1037/0022-3514.92.6.1087 ↗

恆毅力＝對長期目標的堅持與熱情；perseverance of effort 子向度指面對逆境仍持續努力、把事情做完的傾向

  1. I have achieved a goal that took years of work.
  2. I have overcome setbacks to conquer an important challenge.
  3. I finish whatever I begin.
  4. Setbacks don't discourage me.
  5. I am a hard worker.
  6. I am diligent.
量尺：5-point（1 = not at all like me，5 = very much like me）
信度：Study 1（1,545 位 25 歲以上成人）：總量表 α = .85，Consistency of Interests α = .84，Perseverance of Effort α = .78；Study 2 總量表 α = .79
原始情境：成人網路樣本、常春藤大學生、西點軍校學員、全國拼字比賽選手等六個研究
注意：Grit-O 共 12 題，另一子向度 Consistency of Interests 六題皆反向計分：I often set a goal but later choose to pursue a different one／New ideas and new projects sometimes distract me from previous ones／I become interested in new pursuits every few months／My interests change from year to year／I have been obsessed with a certain idea or project for a short time but later lost interest／I have difficulty maintaining my focus on projects that take more than a few months to complete。量表受 APA 版權保護。原文作者以總分為主要指標，但後設分析（Credé et al. 2017）顯示 perseverance of effort 的預測力高於 consistency，單獨用 PE 子量表測「堅持」是常見做法。Grit-S 刪掉 I have achieved a goal that took years of work 與 I have overcome setbacks to conquer an important challenge 兩題。

Duckworth, A. L., & Quinn, P. D.（2009）。Development and validation of the Short Grit Scale (Grit-S)。Journal of Personality Assessment，91(2)，166–174。doi:10.1080/00223890802634290 ↗

  1. Setbacks don't discourage me
  2. I am a hard worker
  3. I finish whatever I begin
  4. I am diligent

Multidimensional Persistence Scale（MPS；Howard & Crayne 2019）

Howard, M. C., & Crayne, M. P.（2019）。Persistence: Defining the multidimensional construct and creating a measure。Personality and Individual Differences，139，77–89。doi:10.1016/j.paid.2018.11.005 ↗

堅持＝為達成目標而忍受困難的傾向，分為 persistence despite difficulty（PDD）、persistence despite fear（PDF）與 inappropriate persistence（IP，為無價值目標仍持續）三向度

  1. PDD（例題）：Even if something is hard, I will keep trying at it.
  2. PDF（例題）：If something is scary, I will do it anyways.
  3. IP（例題）：I will keep trying at something, even if I know my actions are worthless.
量尺：5-point Likert（土耳其版：1 = Kesinlikle Katılmıyorum／strongly disagree，5 = Kesinlikle Katılıyorum／strongly agree）
信度：Howard & Murry（2020，TPM，27(2)，271–277）施測 15 題 MPS：PDF α = .93、PDD α = .83、IP α = .79；Gülbahar 土耳其版（13 題）：PDD .95、PDF .94、IP .80
原始情境：六個研究發展的自陳量表；後續用於成人線上樣本與土耳其大學生
注意：本次只找到三題例題（各向度一題，取自 Howard & Murry 2020 的方法節）。原文 PAID 全文與 ResearchGate 皆被擋，完整題項請回原文附錄或向作者索取。題數在不同來源不一致：Howard & Murry 說 15 題，土耳其版 13 題（PDD 第 1–5 題、PDF 第 6–10 題、IP 第 11–13 題），原文另有 Goal Time Preference 補充量表。改編時注意 IP 是「不當堅持」，與 grit 的 perseverance 方向相反，不能加總；Howard & Murry 證明 Courage Measure 與 PDF 幾乎同構（HTMT = .98）。土耳其版全文：https://acikerisim.gelisim.edu.tr/xmlui/handle/11363/6462

行為式任務堅持：無解字謎（unsolvable anagrams）與習得無助典範

Feather, N. T., & Simon, J. G.（1971）。Attribution of responsibility and valence of outcome in relation to initial confidence。Journal of Personality and Social Psychology，18(2)，173–188；Määttänen, I. 等（2021）。Evidence for a behaviourally measurable perseverance trait in humans。Behavioral Sciences，11(9)，123。doi:10.3390/bs11090123 ↗

堅持＝在無法成功或令人不快的任務上持續投入的時間或嘗試次數

  1. 客觀指標 1：Time on task —— 受試者在無解（或困難）字謎上自願花費的時間（秒），Määttänen 等指出這是文獻中最常用的堅持指標
  2. 客觀指標 2：Attempted solutions —— 測驗階段提交的解答次數（the number of submitted solutions for the test）
  3. 客觀指標 3：Proportion correct —— 可解題的正確比例（the proportion correctly solved test anagrams），用以區分能力與堅持
  4. 程序（Millisecond 版）：5 題練習字謎後接 15 題測驗字謎，其中三分之一無解，每題限時 30 秒，並告知至少答對 8 題才算通過；以練習難度 × 測驗難度做 2×2 設計
量尺：客觀行為指標（秒數、次數、比例）
信度：Määttänen 等（2021，N = 54）以六項行為任務（冷壓、握力、無解字謎、無解語文推理、穿針、無聊影片）得到兩因素結構，共解釋表現時間 37.3% 變異；無解字謎是唯一較受疲勞（depletion）而非特質解釋的任務
原始情境：實驗室個別施測；源自 Seligman 習得無助研究中「先經歷不可控失敗、再觀察是否放棄」的典範
注意：Seligman (1975) 原始論文未在本次開啟，習得無助與字謎堅持的連結取自 Millisecond 與 Määttänen 等的文獻回顧。使用時要注意：(1) 堅持時間會與疲勞、對任務可解性的判斷混淆（在明知無解的題上堅持可能是不理性而非恆毅力）；(2) 需搭配可解題以分離能力；(3) 時間上限（如 30 秒／題）會截斷分布。Behavioral Sciences 全文：https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8468401/
