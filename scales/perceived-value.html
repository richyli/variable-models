Dodds, Monroe & Grewal (1991) Perceived Value（價格—品質—價值模型）

Dodds, W. B., Monroe, K. B., & Grewal, D.（1991）。Effects of price, brand, and store information on buyers' product evaluations。Journal of Marketing Research，28(3)，307–319。doi:10.2307/3172866 ↗

消費者在所示價格下，對產品「付出與所得」的整體權衡判斷（value for the money）

  1. This [product] is a: (very good value for the money – very poor value for the money)
  2. At the price shown this [product] is: (very economical – very uneconomical)
  3. I would consider this [product] to be a good buy. (strongly agree – strongly disagree)
  4. The price shown for this [product] is: (very acceptable – very unacceptable)
  5. This [product] appears to be a bargain. (strongly agree – strongly disagree)
量尺：7-point 語意差異法與 Likert 混用（各題兩端錨定如上）
信度：Dodds 1985 博士論文（同一套工具）報多題指標 Cronbach's α 介於 .892–.958；後續引用研究常報 value 三題版 α ≈ .85
原始情境：5（價格）×3（品牌）×3（商店）受試者間實驗，學生樣本，刺激物為商務用口袋計算機與 SONY Walkman 隨身聽，價格以標示牌呈現
注意：題項逐字取自 Dodds (1985) Virginia Tech 博士論文第 198 頁 Table 41（1991 JMR 文章即由此論文發展，題項相同；[product] 原文為 Business Use Pocket Calculator 或 SONY WALKMAN FM stereo cassette headset player）。同一表另有 willingness to buy 題（The likelihood that I would purchase this … is: very high – very low；If I were going to buy a …, I would consider buying this model at the price shown；At the price shown, I would consider buying the …）。常見縮短版只取前三題（value for the money、economical、good buy）。博士論文 PDF：https://vtechworks.lib.vt.edu/bitstreams/5cd86f45-801f-494c-9db8-c314ee5b0500/download

PERVAL（Sweeney & Soutar 2001）

Sweeney, J. C., & Soutar, G. N.（2001）。Consumer perceived value: The development of a multiple item scale。Journal of Retailing，77(2)，203–220。doi:10.1016/S0022-4359(01)00041-0 ↗

消費者對耐久財的多面向價值知覺，分為品質／績效、情感、價格／金錢價值、社會四個構面

  1. [Quality]
  2. This video game has consistent quality.
  3. This video game is well made.
  4. This video game has an acceptable standard of quality.
  5. This video game would perform consistently.
  6. [Emotional]
  7. This video game is one that I would enjoy.
  8. This video game is one that I would feel relaxed.
  9. This video game would make me feel good.
  10. This video game would give me pleasure.
  11. [Price]
  12. This video game is reasonably priced.
  13. This video game offers value for money.
  14. This video game is a good product for the price.
  15. This video game would be economical.
  16. [Social]
  17. This video game would help me to feel acceptable.
  18. This video game would improve the way I am perceived.
  19. This video game would make a good impression on other people.
  20. This video game would give its owner social approval.
量尺：7-point Likert（strongly disagree – strongly agree）
信度：原文各構面信度未能從開放來源核對
原始情境：澳洲零售消費者評估耐久財（電器等），19 題四構面
注意：本次只找到結構資訊：原版 19 題、四構面（quality、emotional、price/value for money、social）；Walsh, Shiu & Hassan（2014，Journal of Business Research，67(3)，260–267）以原始資料發展 12 題與 8 題短版，Walsh, Kilian & Buxel（2007，Advances in Consumer Research，35，688–689）在德國複製並提 12 題版。逐字題項未在任何可開啟的開放來源中取得（ScienceDirect、ResearchGate、PMC 皆被擋），引用前請從 Journal of Retailing 原文附錄或 Walsh et al. (2014) 附錄抄錄。注意與 Petrick (2002) 的 SERV-PERVAL（服務版，含 is reasonably priced、has outstanding quality 等）是不同工具，不要混用。；補查：不完整且為改編：轉載研究每構面各取 4 題（原量表 quality 6、emotional 5、price 4、social 4 共 19 題），並把主詞改成「This video game」。另一轉載（PMC9022665 線上教育）同樣改編。Journal of Retailing 原文與 Walsh et al. 2014 全文皆未取得開放版本。

Sirdeshmukh, Singh & Sabol (2002) 關係交換中的 Value 四題

Sirdeshmukh, D., Singh, J., & Sabol, B.（2002）。Consumer trust, value, and loyalty in relational exchanges。Journal of Marketing，66(1)，15–37。doi:10.1509/jmkg.66.1.15.18449 ↗

消費者對維持與服務提供者持續關係之「利益減去成本」的知覺（沿用 Zeithaml 1988）

  1. For the prices you pay for clothing items at this store, would you say shopping at this store is a: (Very poor deal – Very good deal)
  2. For the time you spent in order to shop at this store, would you say shopping at this store is: (Highly unreasonable – Highly reasonable)
  3. For the effort involved in shopping at this store, would you say shopping at this store is: (Not at all worthwhile – Very worthwhile)
  4. How you would rate your overall shopping experience at this store? (Extremely poor value – Extremely good value)
量尺：10-point 語意差異法
信度：α = .92（零售與航空兩個情境皆為 .92）
原始情境：零售（常去的百貨公司購買主要服飾，N = 246）與航空（常搭航空公司的非商務旅行，N = 113）兩個關係型服務情境的郵寄問卷
注意：題項逐字取自 MSI Working Paper 01-116（2001）附錄 1（零售版）；附錄 2 航空版把 clothing items at this store 換成 traveling with this airline、shopping 換成 travelling。作者說明題項改編自 Grisaffe & Kumar (1998) 與 Dodds et al. (1991)，四題分別對應價格、時間、心力三種犧牲與整體價值。改編時保留「付出面（價格／時間／心力）＋整體」四題結構即可，10 點量尺可改 7 點。
