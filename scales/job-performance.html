Williams & Anderson 1991 角色內行為（IRB）＋組織公民行為（OCBI／OCBO）

Williams, L. J., & Anderson, S. E.（1991）。Job satisfaction and organizational commitment as predictors of organizational citizenship and in-role behaviors. Journal of Management, 17(3), 601–617 ↗

角色內行為（in-role behavior）指正式工作說明書要求、且為獎酬制度所認可的行為；OCBI 為對特定個人有益的公民行為，OCBO 為對組織整體有益的公民行為。

  1. Adequately completes assigned duties.
  2. Fulfills responsibilities specified in job description.
  3. Performs tasks that are expected of him/her.
  4. Meets formal performance requirements of the job.
  5. Engages in activities that will directly affect his/her performance evaluation.
  6. Neglects aspects of the job he/she is obligated to perform.
  7. Fails to perform essential duties.
  8. Helps others who have been absent.
  9. Helps others who have heavy work loads.
  10. Assists supervisor with his/her work (when not asked).
  11. Takes time to listen to co-workers' problems and worries.
  12. Goes out of way to help new employees.
  13. Takes a personal interest in other employees.
  14. Passes along information to co-workers.
  15. Attendance at work is above the norm.
  16. Gives advance notice when unable to come to work.
  17. Takes undeserved work breaks.
  18. Great deal of time spent with personal phone conversations.
  19. Complains about insignificant things at work.
  20. Conserves and protects organizational property.
  21. Adheres to informal rules devised to maintain order.
量尺：5-point Likert（1 = Strongly disagree … 5 = Strongly agree；整理頁所載，原文由主管評）
信度：整理頁彙整：IRB α = .80–.94、OCBI α = .61–.88、OCBO α = .70–.75；原文 127 位主管評部屬
原始情境：美國企業員工，由直屬主管評定；原始設計為主管他評，後續多改為自評
注意：題項取自二手整理頁（scales.arabpsychology.com），順序為 IRB 7 題（含 2 題反向：Neglects…、Fails…）、OCBI 7 題、OCBO 7 題（含 3 題反向：Takes undeserved work breaks、Great deal of time…、Complains…）。後續 IRT 研究指出 OCBO 的反向題實際上測的是反生產工作行為（CWB）而非 OCB，許多研究因此拿掉這 3 題；短版常只用 IRB 前 4–5 題正向題。自評時「him/her」改「I」。本量表是「工作績效」研究中最常用的角色內績效自評／他評工具。

Goodman & Svyantek 1999 任務績效與脈絡績效量表

Goodman, S. A., & Svyantek, D. J.（1999）。Person–organization fit and contextual performance: Do shared values matter. Journal of Vocational Behavior, 55(2), 254–275 ↗

任務績效（task performance）為職務核心技術工作的熟練程度；脈絡績效（contextual performance）為支持組織社會與心理環境的自發行為，含利他（altruism）與盡責（conscientiousness）兩子面向。

  1. Helps other employees with their work when they have been absent.
  2. Takes initiative to orient new employees to the department even though it is not a part of his/her job description.
  3. Achieves the objectives of the job.
  4. Performs well in the overall job by carrying out tasks as expected.
量尺：7-point Likert（原版；Bağcı & Ünal 2019 土耳其版改 6 點 1 = Strongly Disagree … 6 = Strongly Agree）
信度：原文 α 未能核對；Bağcı & Ünal (2019) 486 位公立醫院員工與其主管自評／他評，MTMM 支持兩因素區辨效度
原始情境：美國企業員工，由同事或主管評；後續常用於自評＋主管評配對設計
注意：只找到 4 題（europeanproceedings 的 MTMM 研究引述的例題），原量表共 25 題：前 16 題為脈絡績效（利他 7 題＋盡責 9 題），後 9 題為任務績效。原文全文（Elsevier）及重印全題項的論文庫均打不開（403），上線前要補齊。常見縮短版只用 altruism 7 題＋task 9 題（共 16 題），盡責面向因與 OCBO 重疊常被拿掉。；補查：不完整：原量表 25 題（前 16 題 contextual、後 9 題 task，見 https://www.europeanproceedings.com/article/10.15405/epsbs.2019.12.03.29 ）；轉載研究 PMC9518503 附錄 Table A1 只列 9 題 task 與 7 題 contextual（利他／altruism 子構面），contextual 的另 9 題（conscientiousness 子構面）未在任何開放來源找到。轉載版本可能有輕微編修（如「Meet criteria」），無法與原文核對。

Individual Work Performance Questionnaire（IWPQ 1.0，Koopmans 2014）

Koopmans, L., Bernaards, C. M., Hildebrandt, V. H., de Vet, H. C. W., & van der Beek, A. J.（2014）。Construct validity of the Individual Work Performance Questionnaire. Journal of Occupational and Environmental Medicine, 56(3), 331–337；美式英文版見 Koopmans, L.（2015）。Individual Work Performance Questionnaire instruction manual. Amsterdam: TNO Innovation for Life – VU University Medical Center ↗

個人工作績效為「與組織目標相關的行為或行動」（Campbell, 1990），分任務績效、脈絡績效、反生產工作行為三個面向，只測行為不測結果。

  1. I was able to plan my work so that I finished it on time.
  2. I kept in mind the work result I needed to achieve.
  3. I was able to set priorities.
  4. I was able to carry out my work efficiently.
  5. I managed my time well.
  6. On my own initiative, I started new tasks when my old tasks were completed.
  7. I took on challenging tasks when they were available.
  8. I worked on keeping my job-related knowledge up-to-date.
  9. I worked on keeping my work skills up-to-date.
  10. I came up with creative solutions for new problems.
  11. I took on extra responsibilities.
  12. I continually sought new challenges in my work.
  13. I actively participated in meetings and/or consultations.
  14. I complained about minor work-related issues at work.
  15. I made problems at work bigger than they were.
  16. I focused on the negative aspects of situation at work instead of the positive aspects.
  17. I talked to colleagues about the negative aspects of my work.
  18. I talked to people outside the organization about the negative aspects of my work.
量尺：5-point，回溯期 past 3 months；任務與脈絡績效：0 = Seldom、1 = Sometimes、2 = Regularly、3 = Often、4 = Always；反生產工作行為：0 = Never、1 = Seldom、2 = Sometimes、3 = Regularly、4 = Often
信度：荷蘭版（n = 1,424）：任務績效 PSI .81／α .78、脈絡績效 PSI .85、CWB PSI .74（手冊 Table 1）；美式英文版 α = .79、.83、.89（三個量表依序）；西班牙版 α = .83、.87、.77（Ramos-Villagrasa et al. 2019）
原始情境：荷蘭各行業員工（藍領、白領、粉領皆有），自評；美式英文版經跨文化調適後在美國工作者驗證
注意：題項為 TNO 手冊第 7 節 American-English version 逐字；荷蘭原版英譯（Koopmans 2014 博士論文）措辭略不同（例：I managed to plan my work so that it was done on time / I complained about unimportant matters at work），引用時要註明用的是哪個版本。原 47 題版還有「適應性績效」面向，1.0 版已併入脈絡績效。三個量表分開計分（各取平均 0–4），不建議加總成單一總分。學術用途免費，商業用途須向 TNO/VUmc 取得授權。
