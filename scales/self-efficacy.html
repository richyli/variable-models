Computer Self-Efficacy（CSE；Compeau & Higgins 1995）

Compeau, D. R., & Higgins, C. A.（1995）。Computer self-efficacy: Development of a measure and initial test. MIS Quarterly, 19(2), 189–211 ↗

個人對自己有能力使用電腦完成工作的判斷（不是指某個特定軟體技能，而是對完成整體任務的能力信念）。

  1. I could complete the job using the software package… if there was no one around to tell me what to do as I go.
  2. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had never used a package like it before.
  3. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had only the software manuals for reference.
  4. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had seen someone else using it before trying it myself.
  5. I could complete the job using the software package… if I could call someone for help if I got stuck.
  6. I could complete the job using the software package… if someone else had helped me get started.
  7. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had a lot of time to complete the job for which the software was provided.
  8. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had just the built-in help facility for assistance.
  9. I could complete the job using the software package… if someone showed me how to do it first.
  10. I could complete the job using the software package… if I had used similar packages before this one to do the same job.
量尺：兩段式：先答 Yes/No（能不能做到），答 Yes 再評信心 1 = Not at all confident、5 = Moderately confident、10 = Totally confident（10 點）
信度：α = .80（Learning Lab 整理頁所載）；Bao et al. (2013) 中國行動學習樣本 α = .911
原始情境：加拿大知識工作者（原文 n ≈ 1,020 位 Financial Post 500 企業經理人與專業人員郵寄問卷）
注意：題項對照 Learning Lab 附件 PDF 與 Bao, Xiong, Hu & Kibelloh (2013, Journal of Educational Computing Research 49(1), 111–132；預印本 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.4211 ）附錄，兩者一致（附件第 4 題漏印 trying，此處依 Bao 版）。Marakas 等人批評此量表測的是「一般 CSE」，與特定任務的「特定 CSE」要分開；研究手機 App 或 AI 工具時通常自編特定 CSE 題（I believe I have the ability to …）。多數後續研究省略 Yes/No 那一段，直接用 10 點信心評分。題幹 software package 可依情境改成具體系統名。

General Self-Efficacy Scale（GSE；Schwarzer & Jerusalem 1995）

Schwarzer, R., & Jerusalem, M.（1995）。Generalized Self-Efficacy scale. In J. Weinman, S. Wright, & M. Johnston (Eds.), Measures in health psychology: A user's portfolio. Causal and control beliefs (pp. 35–37). Windsor, UK: NFER-NELSON ↗

一般性自我效能：相信自己能應付各領域中新的或困難的任務、能因應逆境的樂觀自我信念。

  1. I can always manage to solve difficult problems if I try hard enough.
  2. If someone opposes me, I can find the means and ways to get what I want.
  3. It is easy for me to stick to my aims and accomplish my goals.
  4. I am confident that I could deal efficiently with unexpected events.
  5. Thanks to my resourcefulness, I know how to handle unforeseen situations.
  6. I can solve most problems if I invest the necessary effort.
  7. I can remain calm when facing difficulties because I can rely on my coping abilities.
  8. When I am confronted with a problem, I can usually find several solutions.
  9. If I am in trouble, I can usually think of a solution.
  10. I can usually handle whatever comes my way.
量尺：4-point：1 = Not at all true、2 = Hardly true、3 = Moderately true、4 = Exactly true；加總 10–40，無反向題
信度：作者網頁：23 國樣本 α = .76–.90，多數在 .80 後段；德國五個樣本 α = .82–.93；Scholz et al. (2002) 25 國 n = 19,120 國際平均 29.55
原始情境：一般成人與青少年；原為 1979 年德文 20 題版，1981 年縮為 10 題，英文版 1995 年出版；已有 30 餘種語言版（含中文）
注意：題項取自作者 Ralf Schwarzer 的 FU Berlin 官方頁；PsyToolkit 版（ https://psytoolkit.org/survey-library/generalized-self-efficacy-gse.html ）第 3、5、8、9、10 題措辭略有出入（少了 usually 等字），引用時以作者頁為準。非商業研究免申請授權，但要註明出處。有些研究改成 5 點或 7 點，與常模比較時要換算。中文版已由 Zhang & Schwarzer (1995) 發表。

New General Self-Efficacy Scale（NGSE；Chen, Gully & Eden 2001）

Chen, G., Gully, S. M., & Eden, D.（2001）。Validation of a new general self-efficacy scale. Organizational Research Methods, 4(1), 62–83 ↗

個人對自己在各種情境中達成表現的整體能力信念（one's belief in one's overall competence to effect requisite performances across a wide variety of achievement situations）。

  1. I will be able to achieve most of the goals that I have set for myself.
  2. When facing difficult tasks, I am certain that I will accomplish them.
  3. In general, I think that I can obtain outcomes that are important to me.
  4. I believe I can succeed at most any endeavor to which I set my mind.
  5. I will be able to successfully overcome many challenges.
  6. I am confident that I can perform effectively on many different tasks.
  7. Compared to other people, I can do most tasks very well.
  8. Even when things are tough, I can perform quite well.
量尺：5-point Likert：1 = Strongly disagree … 5 = Strongly agree；取平均
信度：二手整理頁報 α = .85–.90（scales.arabpsychology.com）；原文兩國（美國、以色列）多個樣本信度高、且比 Sherer 等人的 SGSE 更單向度，但原文確切 α 無法從可開啟頁面核對
原始情境：美國與以色列大學生與軍人（管理／組織心理學情境），用來預測各種任務的特定自我效能
注意：題項對照 SPARQ 與 UCSD EMERGE 兩頁一致（EMERGE 第 1 題為 that I have set、第 4 題為 almost any；SPARQ 第 1 題 that I set、第 4 題 most any——以原文為準者建議採 EMERGE 版）。與 GSE 的差別：NGSE 由 14 題池篩出 8 題、單因素、題目全為「能達成目標／克服挑戰」的 can-do 語氣；GSE 混有因應（coping）成分。組織行為研究偏用 NGSE，健康心理學偏用 GSE。

Bandura 自我效能量表編製指引（領域專屬量表的通用格式）

Bandura, A.（2006）。Guide for constructing self-efficacy scales. In F. Pajares & T. Urdan (Eds.), Self-efficacy beliefs of adolescents (Vol. 5, pp. 307–337). Greenwich, CT: Information Age Publishing ↗

自我效能是對自己「能夠」（can do）組織並執行達成特定成果所需行動的能力判斷，必須針對特定功能領域量身編題，不存在通用題項。

  1. The items should be phrased in terms of can do rather than will do. Can is a judgment of capability; will is a statement of intention.（Bandura 2006, p. 308）
  2. Participants record the strength of their efficacy beliefs on a 100-point scale, ranging in 10-unit intervals from 0 (Cannot do); through intermediate degrees of assurance, 50 (Moderately certain can do); to complete assurance, 100 (Highly certain can do).（Bandura 2006, p. 312）
量尺：0–100、每 10 分一格（0 = Cannot do；50 = Moderately certain can do；100 = Highly certain can do）；亦可用 0–10 的 11 點版，但 Bandura 反對 5 點或 7 點（區辨力不足）
信度：不適用（指引非固定量表）
原始情境：方法指引；範例量表（學業、自我調節學習、社交等）附於該章
注意：本條不是量表，而是編題規則；Bandura 原章 PDF 各鏡像（uky.edu、emory.edu）已失效，引文取自 Reading Matrix 期刊文章（Wyatt 2021 評述）逐字引用的頁碼。編題要點：題目列出不同難度等級的任務（graded difficulty）、避免「will」、避免天花板效應、先讓受試者練習評分、量表要與結果變項的領域對齊。學生常見錯誤是拿 GSE 這類一般量表去預測特定行為，Bandura 明確反對。
