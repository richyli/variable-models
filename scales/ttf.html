TTF1 份量表↗ 在理論模型裡看這個變數

別名：Task-Technology Fit、Task Technology Fit

變數群：任務與科技特性　›　科技特性　用到它的理論：TTF

Goodhue & Thompson (1995) 題項未對照原文

Goodhue & Thompson (1995), MIS Quarterly 19(2), 213–236：8 構面（quality, locatability, authorization, compatibility, ease of use/training, production timeliness, systems reliability, relationship with users）；後續多用「fit-as-match」短版（如 Lin & Huang 2008 的 8 題） ↗

  1. [Fit-as-match 短版] The functions of [system] fit my task requirements.
  2. [Fit-as-match] Using [system] fits well with the way I work.
  3. [Fit-as-match] [System] is adequate / compatible for my tasks.
  4. [Goodhue 構面例, quality] The data that I use or would like to use is accurate enough for my purposes.
  5. [Goodhue 構面例, locatability] It is easy to locate the data I need.
  6. [Goodhue 構面例, timeliness] The information I need is available in time.
量尺：7-point Likert, strongly disagree – strongly agree
信度：Goodhue & Thompson 各構面 α 多在 .70–.90（未核對）；短版 α 常見 .85 以上
